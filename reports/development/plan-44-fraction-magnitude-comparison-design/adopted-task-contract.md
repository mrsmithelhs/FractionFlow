# Plan 44 adopted comparison task

Date: 2026-10-31. The owner explicitly adopted the revised task with ONE CHOSEN
MODEL PER PROBLEM and directed closure of Plan44 and preparation of its source
packet. This contract supersedes the dossier's model-switching recommendation
and remaining-adoption language. Earlier versions remain design history.

## Adopted learner behavior

- Start with both original fractions in stacked notation and no bars. One direct
  <, =, > selection with full spoken meaning and deliberate Check is the response.
  Keep source expressions visible once; no separate verbal comparison question
  or required conversion/encounter acknowledgment.
- One optional help disclosure contains reasoning/notation help and source-model
  choice. The learner may choose either source fraction to model, but once chosen,
  that choice is fixed for this problem attempt until acceptance. Help closing/
  reopening, wrong Check, view changes, resizing and other remounts do not unlock
  or replace it. Do not display an empty second bar as if it were zero.
- After acceptance, show both honest equal-whole aligned models as verification,
  retain the accepted symbolic relation, then offer direct Next problem or final
  Back to start. Next and an explicit fresh-problem restart reset model choice,
  response/recovery and assistance state according to existing fresh-state rules.
- Requested help is assistance, not evidence of independent reasoning or mastery.
  Use a concrete next-step cue for close/equivalent cases without supplying the
  correct relation or requiring a new conversion-response beat. Never use
  misleading whole sizes, reversed shading or a visual trick to obscure amounts.
- Retain existing More/Less support only: same task/batch/model limit and access;
  More may offer one concise strategy cue, Less leaves the same assistance on
  request. Project support consequences through instructional state and scene.

The adopted initial content proposal is the revised 12 ordered positive-proper
fraction pairs in comparison-design.md/evidence/exact-examples.mjs, with four
cases per relation, source denominators <=10, equality, swaps and structural/
benchmark/counterexample coverage. They are not yet registered production data.
Derive truth/eligibility and contextual hint values using exact APIs; preserve
unreduced source forms. Browser readability and accessible interaction still
require source-delivery evidence and owner rendered acceptance.

Benchmark-line comparison, unsigned distance, mixed/improper values, larger
denominator admission, strategy choosers and adaptation remain separate work.
No source implementation, new replay version, push or deployment is authorized
by adopting this design; the next packet's mechanism gate remains intact.
