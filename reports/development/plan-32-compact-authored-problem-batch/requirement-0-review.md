   a universal yes/no cue across new items. Propose explicit stable answer positions

   revision scheme, with reconstruction lookup and a small import/data-flow outline.
   Avoid materializer/accessor/validator cycles or duplicated discovery.
4. Inspect current denominator/conversion help for targets 10/20/15/30; renderer
   eligibility alone does not establish help closure. Bring any additional bounds
   back before implementation approval. Keep known keyboard recovery limitations

make these choices concrete. No source/tests/registry/bank activation is authorized.
