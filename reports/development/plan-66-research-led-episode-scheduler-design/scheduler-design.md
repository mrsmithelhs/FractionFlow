## Recommendation and comparison

Recommend a later pilot limited to **symbolic comparison of proper fractions**, using existing admitted Plan45/49 records and the learner's existing support/help. Begin with a short authored nonadaptive sequence; add only a voluntary, within-visit **offer** after a valid first retry or accepted response: continue the planned item or choose an available same-purpose alternative. The first pilot should not make cross-visit inferences or change support automatically. The opportunity to choose is an untested [H] design safety and agency measure, not a demonstrated learning intervention. If a meaningful alternate admitted item or interface cannot be verified, retain the authored next item or finish; do not invent a task or an easier/harder ranking.

| Direction | Operation and scope | Benefits and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Authored nonadaptive comparison baseline | An explicitly chosen, finite sequence of admitted items and task-local support, with ordinary deliberate Next, help, skip/exit and no history requirement. The same sequence is used irrespective of responses. | Explainable comparator; may itself be useful. It makes no claim that its sequence, length or support causes learning. |
| B. Within-visit, response-informed offers **(recommended hypothesis)** | Same catalog and initial sequence; on a valid retry offer existing help and, after the learner resolves or leaves an episode, let the learner select the planned next item or an authored compatible alternative when available. Session-local observations can qualify a concise reason; learner choice overrides. No automatic fading, level, labels, promotion, required remediation or cross-visit selection. | Tests a small additional policy and its burden. The offer could distract, be ignored or create choice overload; it needs an independently evaluated comparison with A. Learner-controlled help already exists; any extra offer or selection is future work, not current behavior. |

A reviewable **candidate rule** for the later pilot is: start with the same authored 02 → 08 → 11 outline in both conditions. In B only, if 02 has a valid first retry, offer at its resolution or explicit exit a choice of planned 08 or the pre-admitted 09 as the second slot; if 02 is first accepted, continue to the planned 08 without a response-triggered offer. Choosing 09 replaces 08, it does not append a fourth task; after either choice the outline returns to 11 and then ends. This single optional offer per session bounds repeated remediation. Selecting the planned item, declining, skipping or exiting never increases demands; ordinary task help remains independently available. A learner-requested different goal is handled through available navigation, not counted as a response. If 09 is unavailable or not approved at the mechanism gate, omit that branch and use A. The live instructional transition supplies the retry event in memory even when remembering is off; the event and offer are discarded at session end and do not require Plan63 storage. Item 09 preserves symbolic comparison while changing from a shared denominator to a shared numerator; its proposed purpose is to offer a different valid way to practice the same broad responsibility, not remediation or an assumed easier item. Whether that change is suitable after a retry needs owner and learner review. This is a proposed comparison of a package (offer plus item choice) against fixed ordering; it cannot isolate the value of choice from the changed item. A separately approved evaluation should address that confound, and should not assume the items are equivalent in difficulty.

Neither approach assumes a diagnostic inference from one choice. P2 supports a qualified investigation of carefully constructed fraction practice, not automatic switching; P1/P3 show system packages are confounded; P4 suggests a specific reward/re-practice combination can be harmful, not that choice itself is harmful. P5/P6 do not establish equivalent learning or applicability to children. P7 warns against importing struggle labels. Project prior research identifies transfer failures and calls for stable practice before deliberate variation. The zero-history baseline remains a complete usable route.

## Decomposition and invariants

1. **Catalog metadata:** authored and reviewed purpose, admitted item/recipe and revisions, relation structure, representation and actual support/help capability. Relation structure is descriptive, not a learner difficulty score. Preserve exact item eligibility and meaning.
2. **Observations:** valid instructional response classifications and known assistance snapshots, explicitly conditioned by support and actual task identity. Invalid/blank/no-op/technical events are not math failures.
3. **Tentative evidence:** factual, local and task-specific observations; unknown is unknown. Distinct items, reversed pairs and cues must not be treated as independent breadth automatically. No stable strength/weakness or mastery label from this pilot.
4. **Practice policy:** deterministic offer eligibility and learner override rules, with finite authored opportunities and one concise reason. The policy proposes; the learner may decline without penalty. A learner's override is a choice, not a deficit or evidence of mastery.
5. **Episode/item selection:** select only an admitted compatible record before episode construction; preserve its complete frozen schedule, task-local help and exact math truth. No LLM, arbitrary beat omission or renderer-calculated correctness.
6. **Session lifecycle:** deliberate start/Next, retry same identity, fresh install only after successful preparation, interruption/exit and natural final return. Unfinished navigation and draft are ephemeral, as in Plan57/60; history is not resume.

Purpose (introduction, supported practice, review, evidence check) is an authored intent separate from the selected recipe and representation. In the proposed small pool, the same comparison recipe could serve a supported practice or a less-cued check, but **only** where its actual support configuration makes that distinction truthful. A check is an opportunity to observe, not proof of understanding. Introduction should use an already available, appropriate experience; no new teaching scene is assumed. A later return after other work is within-visit order, not verified day-based spacing.

Challenge, help availability, representation familiarity, denominator capacity and inferred competence are independent. A larger denominator does not automatically mean harder; choosing More support, changing a visual/linear access path or requesting help must never become an ability label. ZPD is an instructional aspiration, not a numerical property obtained from clicks. Keep examples and hints available subject to actual task contracts; Plan56 owns unresolved support/independence policy and must be coordinated before any fading design.

## Concrete illustrative visits

These are proposed flows, not observed learners. The actual catalog contains `plan45-compare-02` (5/8, 3/8), `plan45-compare-08` (the reversed pair) and `plan45-compare-11` (4/8, 1/2). Plan57's accepted four-episode session includes 02, 08, 11 and a distinct distance task; the proposed comparison-only pilot is a new separately approved session design, not a silent replacement. Exact truth stays in math APIs; an implementation packet must validate record admission, support and pair relationships.

- **Visit 1, no history:** learner starts the authored comparison sequence at 02. A valid retry leads to existing corrective feedback and optional help. If they use a model and then succeed, describe the event as a supported successful comparison, not independent mastery. After the resolved retry, the candidate offer is planned 08 or, if separately reviewed for this pilot, 09 (3/5, 3/7); this availability is a proposal requiring admission and support review, not a claim of an existing menu. The learner may choose either, continue unaided, or finish; no approved alternate means continue or finish. They choose the planned 08. This reverses the same pair and is practice, not new breadth evidence. They may leave; no unfinished session is resumed.
- **Visit 2, same browser or a different person:** the pilot starts from a chosen authored entry without assuming who used Visit 1 or when. The learner begins at 02 and answers correctly on the first valid response, so B makes no response-triggered offer; the planned 08 and then 11 follow. They can request existing Help or a model and can leave by the normal route. The installed More/Less profile remains fixed under the current episode/session contract; requesting Help is not a profile change. After 11 the session ends at its authored finite boundary; no continuation offer is implied. When B offers the alternate after a retry, its concise reason is “You can try another comparison.” It does not call the earlier performance stale, decide the learner forgot, or require a harder problem.

A future version might use a learner-confirmed starting suggestion from the existing record, but only after a separate owner-approved identity/uncertainty contract; the present pilot excludes that input. Asking for help, selecting another goal, choosing available easier/harder work, keeping the same kind, skip/pause/exit, or refusing a recommendation carries no penalty. No pressure, reward or duration target. Alternative labels must reflect real authored capabilities; do not promise a representation switch or worked example that the selected task does not offer.

## Synthetic adversarial histories: policy bounds, not test results

| Input scenario | Expected bound | Forbidden inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No data, remembering off | Use authored choice and finite sequence; no storage prerequisite. | Weakness or beginner identity. |
| Model used then accepted | Offer existing support or planned next; retain help context if eligible. | Independent mastery, failed learning or automatic support removal. |
| 02 and 08 repeatedly accepted | Recognize authored reversal as the same pair for evidence interpretation; avoid presenting repetition as breadth. | Transfer, automatic promotion or a streak threshold. |
| Correct then retry on a different item | Preserve both as observations; offer choice without irreversible classification. | Stable decline, universal weakness or ignoring contradiction. |
| Many comparison records but no distance observations | Give no advantage to the better-observed concept as an overall score; keep distance outside pilot. | Evidence of arithmetic or distance competence. |
| Wrong challenge guess; learner chooses another available goal or restores support | Respect the choice immediately, without penalty; keep the current episode's valid state unless learner exits. | That refusal or support choice is failure. |
| Old, mixed-user, cleared or unavailable history | No history-based selection or identity claim; normal authored entry and B’s in-memory offer remain available. | Age of data, forgetting or a single owner's ability. |
| Session interrupted during retry | Preserve only Plan63's already-admitted eligible observation if remembering is enabled; unfinished session is not resumed. | Completion, hidden saved position or an extra error. |
| No compatible alternate or unsupported representation | Offer only available authored path or finish; never require unavailable controls. | That every task has every help, representation or accessibility affordance. |
| Assisted repeated success followed by a less-cued error | Keep assistance and different cue context explicit; allow a chosen supported opportunity. | A simple count or global score can resolve competence, or remove support automatically. |

## Later implementation and evaluation gate

A separate packet would first pin catalog eligibility, exact paths and policy ownership; likely app-level session composition consumes validated task/recipe contexts, an interaction-level policy consumes explicitly admitted observations, and the storage edge remains the Plan63 adapter rather than math/render. These are boundaries, not assigned source changes. Version task/recipe, evidence interpretation, catalog and policy meaning. Reject unknown or incompatible revisions, quarantine rather than silently reinterpret assisted history, and recheck Plan63 record coverage and copy for every altered task/session. A generation-bound episode token and existing transaction deduplication govern persisted comparison activity; ephemeral offer state is tied to the active session and retired on exit, restart or failed preparation; it is independent of Plan63 storage readiness and generation. Clear invalidates old persisted observations and writers but does not itself erase or gate an in-memory offer; the offer contains no persisted record or personal identity and cannot recreate erased entries. If ties use a seed, record its scope and make reconstruction deterministic; prefer an authored stable order for this slice. Validate failure, reset, invalid responses, two-tab clear and no-history fallback in the actual mechanism before implementation.

Evaluation would compare B against A with the same admitted content, supports, opportunity to leave and as comparable a practice exposure as practical. Separately approved independent checks should include immediate and delayed less-cued comparison on new pairs, deliberately varied structure, and a distinct transfer task; also collect learner-reported burden and whether the offer is understood or rejected. Predefine missing-data, shared-device and interruption handling, allocation, outcome measures and uncertainty. Practice accuracy, time, completion and synthetic histories are not evidence of retention or engagement. No learner recruitment, instrumentation, data collection or deployment is authorized by this report.

## Owner choices and revisit triggers

1. **Initial purpose and baseline:** recommend comparison-only, nonadaptive authored A as the control; choose which compatible items and short finite length after reviewing actual workload. Four in Plan57 is an adopted session dose, not a general threshold.
2. **Policy direction:** recommend B as an optional within-visit offer, no auto-support or mastery, only after a separate implementation mechanism and pilot approval. A alone is appropriate if there is no useful reviewed alternative or the choice is burdensome.
3. **History:** recommend no cross-visit selector input in first pilot; preserve Plan63 optional descriptive history and compatibility. Any date or broader task observation requires a separately justified, minimal extension.
4. **Help and agency:** retain learner control and real task support, reject fixed streaks, forced challenge and provider-derived three-error cutoff. Defer automatic fading to a reconciled Plan56 contract.
5. **Evaluation:** owner decides whether and when a separately approved learner study and independent outcome comparison are warranted; no educational benefit is claimed now.

A later contract may cautiously explore tentative, purpose-specific strengths and practice needs from varied, assistance-qualified observations; extend coverage to other tasks only with their own admission contracts; and offer cross-visit suggestions only after addressing shared-browser ownership, elapsed time if needed, compatibility and explicit learner confirmation. Those are owner ambitions and research questions, not an authorized roadmap commitment or a consequence of this pilot.

Revisit if the actual admitted pool cannot supply meaningful non-repeated alternatives, a source contradicts a decision-carrying claim, a proposed observation cannot be captured truthfully, Plan56 resolves different support semantics, or learner-evaluation results show burdensome choice or no advantage.
