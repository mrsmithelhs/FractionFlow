## Recommendation and comparison

Recommend **one final two-field response**, with a learner-controlled renaming decision available as optional, explicitly requested help. This carries forward the compact Plan 78 response while making the exchange available when useful. It is a design judgment: the direct response alone does not establish whether a learner noticed or understood decomposition, and no learner observation here establishes that the optional step is sufficient. If the owner wants evidence of a prompted exchange on every item, choose the required-decision candidate instead; learner observations showing that optional help is overlooked or that a required step creates unproductive friction would also warrant reconsideration. Neither outcome establishes transfer by itself.

| Aspect | A: direct final response | B: required renaming decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Same starting task and aids | Original operands, equal-scale starting amounts, blank whole and eighth fields, fixed `/ 8`, same unit help and Check | Same operands, models and unit help; same final fields and Check after the exchange decision |
| Learner action | Enter final whole and eighth counts and Check once | Enter the total eighths after one whole is renamed, validate it, then enter the same final fields and Check |
| Before final Check | Original quantities, learner draft and any requested unit help only. No computed exchange, removal, or result. | After a correct entry, show the remaining whole count and the entered total eighth count, with the eight renamed pieces distinguished from the original pieces. Show no removal or result. This reveals the renamed minuend and therefore gives arithmetic information that A does not; compare that difference explicitly. |
| Inference | A submission and help requests can be observed; strategy and understanding are unknown. | A correct prompted count can be observed; it does not show that the learner chose to decompose unaided or understands conservation. |
| Cost | Minimal interruption, but the exchange is not a required contribution. | A focused contribution, at the cost of a gate and disclosed intermediate information. |

For the recommended optional version, a learner chooses **Rename one whole**; it opens the same brief entry as B. The action is not a prerequisite for final Check. Opening it counts as requested task help even if abandoned or answered incorrectly; preserve that fact across retries and view changes for the attempt, if a future recording design supports it. Do not describe a completed attempt as independent merely because no help was requested. Show no unrequested solution, do not change support settings, and do not introduce automatic adaptation. A direct attempt with no request is *unassisted in the app*, not proof of independent thought. Assistance semantics follow the [Plan 56 contract](../../../docs/development/plan-56-task-specific-support-and-independence-design.md); local record eligibility and schema changes are outside this proposal.

In B or optional help, for `3 2/8`, show `2 wholes and [ ] eighths` after explaining that one of the three wholes is being renamed. The learner supplies `10`. Validate an integer against `8 + 2`; an empty field is incomplete, malformed or out-of-range is invalid, and another integer is a nonmatching exchange. Retain the entry, give a concise prompt such as “One whole is 8 eighths. Count all the eighths,” and allow editing and another check without revealing the total. A valid entry shows `2 wholes and 10 eighths`; it does not automatically subtract. Editing a response clears stale feedback without erasing the other field. In the optional path, closing help retains draft and assistance history, and reopening restores its state. Do not leave a spent confirmation button after success; move focus to the final response, or to a clear continuation target when appropriate. Fresh attempt and Next reset attempt-local drafts and help; a future implementation must define replay compatibility before claiming it.

Final fields start blank; both must contain explicit nonnegative integer counts. For `2/8`, enter whole `0` and eighths `2`; blank is incomplete. Preserve eighths rather than requiring reduction. On a nonmatching final amount, retain both fields and give a retry message without revealing the answer; distinguish invalid and incomplete drafts. If an equivalent unorganized form is allowed in a later implementation, give organization feedback instead of calling it unequal, and specify bounds and classification before source work. Do not silently classify out-of-bounds equivalents as unequal. Accepted results use organized wholes and 0–7 eighths. The control with zero eighths belongs to the no-decomposition family, not this live batch.

## Proposed compact batch and exact evidence

Write an authored record as positive mixed operands `W a/8 − V b/8`, with integer `W > V >= 1`, `1 <= a < b <= 7`; one whole is renamed. The new count is `8+a`, and the organized result is `(W−V−1)` wholes and `8+a−b` eighths. The possible remainders under these bounds are **2–7**, not 1–7: the largest deficit is six. One eighth requires a source boundary excluded here; zero eighths requires a no-decomposition control.

| Proposed order | Problem | Exchange | Final fields | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | `3 2/8 − 1 5/8` | `2 10/8` | `1`, `5` | Required mixed-result witness |
| B | `2 1/8 − 1 7/8` | `1 9/8` | `0`, `2` | Below one; smallest admitted remainder; explicit zero whole |
| C | `4 3/8 − 2 7/8` | `3 11/8` | `1`, `4` | More wholes and widest proposed intermediate; distinct result |
| D | `2 2/8 − 1 3/8` | `1 10/8` | `0`, `7` | Below one; largest admitted remainder |

C replaces `4 3/8 − 2 6/8`, whose `1 5/8` result duplicated A. Larger quantities still exercise a distinct representation but the replacement adds result variety. This is a coverage choice, not a claim of greater learning. Batch membership and order require owner adoption. A separate no-decomposition control, `3 3/8 − 1 3/8 = 2 0/8`, is not a live item. The excluded `1 2/8 − 2 5/8` is negative (`−1 3/8` mathematically); the nonnegative core compares the operands and rejects the subtraction rather than representing the negative value.

Run `node reports/development/plan-80-mixed-number-subtraction-with-decomposition-design/exact-check.mjs` from the repository root. It uses the existing exact core to compare each original and renamed value, check the specified renamed form and subtraction both before and after exchange, check the no-decomposition control and negative rejection, and enumerate the fractional numerator boundary. This verifies exact arithmetic for these witnesses, not admission or a running interface. See [exact check](exact-check.mjs).

## Visual and linear state sketches

Use equal-width whole units and eight equal cells per unit. The exchanged whole occupies the same width before and after splitting. Original fractional pieces, the eight exchanged pieces, removed pieces, and empty space outside the starting amount must be distinguishable with labels or patterns as well as color. Do not require drag, animation, or color perception. Use the same mathematical content in a linear reading order.

| State | Mixed result: A | Below one: B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial, both designs | Visual: three equal whole units plus two marked eighths; separately identify `1 5/8` to subtract. Linear: “Start with 3 wholes and 2 eighths. Take away 1 whole and 5 eighths.” Blank final fields. | Visual: two whole units plus one marked eighth; identify `1 7/8` to subtract. Linear names those quantities; blank fields. No precomputed remaining count in either accessible name or hidden content. |
| Intermediate, required or requested renaming | One whole splits in place into eight marked cells; learner enters total eighths. After valid entry: 2 intact wholes, 8 renamed plus 2 original eighths, labeled `2 wholes and 10 eighths`. No removal shown. | One whole splits in place; after valid entry: 1 intact whole, 8 renamed plus 1 original eighth, labeled `1 whole and 9 eighths`. No removal shown. |
| Recovery | Preserve entry and help state; feedback identifies incomplete, invalid, or nonmatching work. Visual and linear paths describe starting quantities and submitted draft without computed answer. A wrong exchange remains editable. | Same, including explicit distinction between blank whole field and entered `0`. No final remainder model before acceptance. |
| Accepted | Show the exchange, the removal of 1 whole and 5 eighths, and the remaining 1 whole and 5 eighths, with complete count description. | Show the exchange, removal of 1 whole and 7 eighths, and remaining 0 wholes and 2 eighths; accepted notation `2/8`. |

For the direct design, exchange and removal first appear after acceptance. For the decision design, the exchange appears only after a valid intermediate entry; the starting amount must remain traceable throughout. Initial visual and accessible descriptions must not calculate the result. Equal-width illustrations and complete linear counts are design requirements, not rendered observations. When an entire model cannot fit legibly, supply a complete labeled count fallback for starting, exchanged, removed and remaining quantities as applicable, rather than clipping or shrinking cells into illegibility.

## Follow-on slice and validation gates

The smallest proposed source slice is one standalone same-denominator eighths subtraction family: compact operand records and explicit versioned set membership, task-specific admission/materialization using exact math, two-field state and optional-help provenance, and equal-scale visual/linear presentation. Reuse the [Plan 77](../../../docs/development/plan-77-mixed-addition-modular-authoring-pilot.md) separation where suitable without importing an addition-specific validator; use [Plan 78](../../../docs/development/plan-78-mixed-number-subtraction-without-decomposition.md) as the direct no-decomposition comparison and [Plan 74](../../../docs/development/plan-74-mixed-number-addition-with-regrouping.md) as the reverse equivalence precedent. Before writing source, inventory shared files, route/chooser impacts, existing records and replay, identities, precise response bounds, support and reset behavior; escalate protected migrations. No scheduler admission, new persistence, unlike denominators, adaptation, or existing task changes are implied.

A later implementation should verify admission and exclusions, conservation, both result forms, blank versus zero, invalid and equivalent drafts, optional-help provenance, retry and view-switch state, focus, linear/keyboard/non-drag completion, reduced motion, progression and replay. At **360 × 740**, check normal and **200% root text** for A and B and the widest intermediate C; inspect initial, recovery, intermediate and accepted states for both result forms, suffix, full counts, equal unit/cell widths, accessible names, controls and overflow. Vertical scrolling is acceptable; the fallback must convey all counts. Also check a representative Chromebook width and state which input/device checks are emulated. Root-text scaling is not browser zoom. No browser, physical-device, assistive-technology or learner observation was performed for this design.
