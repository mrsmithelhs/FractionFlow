# Plan 87 — second fit evidence checkpoint

2026-11-07. **Evidence and proposed refusal boundary, not approved fit thresholds.** The first checkpoint is `measurement-checkpoint.md`. No production renderer was changed; normal standalone and managed entry, registered bank exposure, records and replay are unchanged.

`measure-journeys.mjs` mounted actual registered records at positions 1, 15 and 30 in each of four Plan 89 banks through the report-local app and drove 72 complete single-item journeys: three layout configurations × four banks × three records × visual/linear. It used the real controls and checked the registered identity, answer completion, and an entered draft before and after a 320px-wide resize, restoring the original width before answering. All 72 reached resolved and draft/identity assertions passed; the raw per-stage geometry and individual identities are in `journey-observations.json`. These samples were chosen to span the registration list and Plan 40's construct, inspect and identify recipes; they are not a proof of worst-case geometry for every item. Each mode was tested separately, not as an in-episode view-toggle, and focus, speech, keyboard navigation, help, actual browser zoom and physical devices were not verified. CSS root enlargement is 200% text simulation. No page errors were recorded. Because answer submission followed restoration, this demonstrates retained draft, not a usable continuation at 320px.

At 360px viewport and 32px root text, the three Plan 78 samples show 9px document overflow in both modes at initial and shrink captures. The initial overflowing elements include `.plan58-label`, `.plan74-piece-entry`, and `.plan74-denominator`, with the latter reaching x=369px. In sampled Plan 40 journeys the operation fraction controls overflowed in the enlarged configuration; one visual sample measured document overflow of 139px after the transform, with `.control-fraction-fields`, its label and `operate-fraction-denominator-input` extending to x=471.1px. Geometry and layout are observations, not a full causal analysis or evidence that every item has identical overflow. None of the sampled Plan 74 or 82 captures reported document overflow, but that alone does not certify their participation or all records. The report does not infer that wide normal or narrow normal is universally safe.

`measure-managed.mjs` separately ran 12 ordinary Start journeys across seeds 0 and 3, the same three layouts, and visual/linear. It completed the two comparison controls and reached the initial distance in each, observing the expected five possible registered IDs (`plan45-compare-02`, `plan45-compare-09`, `plan45-compare-10`, `distance-01`, `distance-04`); the captured screens had no document-level horizontal overflow. It did not complete distance, test help, resize/draft/focus or establish a fit class. `managed-observations.json` holds the measurements. Neither run tests AT or learner understanding.

`draw-smoke.mjs` separately mounted a seed-zero four-item ordered draw from each real registered bank through the ordinary report-local chooser and confirmed the first selected identity launched; it did not traverse Next, Retry or resize replacement. `plan87-draw.js` implements a deterministic selection candidate and pure suffix recheck with a bounded four-item maximum; `selectFittingPracticeSession` is a separate unconnected Plan 70 slot helper. Focused selector and existing scheduler/replay tests passed as recorded in the first checkpoint; the candidate is not the completed in-app feature.

**Proposed conservative policy for this gate:** the verified allow-list remains empty for both expanded-bank and managed capability selection. Unknown/zero/stale measurement and all other unverified conditions refuse a capability-selected launch, leaving the ordinary entry usable. Specifically, exclude 360px/32px Plan 78 and Plan 40 visual/linear paths from a claimed fit class because of observed overflow. Do not extrapolate a breakpoint or claim a usable shrink continuation: if the review harness cannot establish an equivalent participation path while preserving active state, retain the item/draft and refuse progression or offer recovery, rather than silently replacing it. This empty policy is a proposed safe boundary, not an implemented recovery UI or approval to change ordinary entry. A synthetic fixture may later demonstrate different eligibility and replacement without making production claims.

**Request to orchestration:** review the findings and proposed empty allow-list/refusal boundary; direct whether a separately scoped production renderer repair and its gate should precede fit classification, or whether to continue isolated fixture-only selection/resize work. No threshold, numerical expansion, default rollout or new replay schema is adopted. Owner rendered review and separate chooser exposure approval remain open.
