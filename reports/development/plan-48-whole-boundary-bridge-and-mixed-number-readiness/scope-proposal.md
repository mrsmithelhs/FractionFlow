## Candidates to compare

- **A: [0,2] locate-and-verify bridge (provisional preference).** Given a bounded original fraction, the learner independently locates its quantity on a two-whole line; after acceptance, a stable equal-unit multi-whole bar verifies the same quantity. Initial placement and any source model must not reveal the answer. The dossier will determine which forms and bounds are supportable and whether this remains a short, accessible task.
- **B: whole-plus-fraction construction.** The learner constructs a quantity using stable equal wholes and a fractional part, then connects it to a supplied or learner-established improper form. The dossier must explicitly identify which form is earned and avoid presenting a conversion as independent reasoning when it was supplied.

Recommend only one after checking evidence. Neither candidate assumes mixed-number operations or establishes a Phase 4 exit. Both require meaningful learner work, concise feedback, accessible alternatives and no arbitrary preliminary Continue.

## Falsifiers and uncertainty

- Reject or revise a unit/geometry claim if current source or evidence shows a bar whole mapped to the entire two-whole line, unequal scale across wholes or representations, boundary off-by-one behavior, or correctness derived from viewport geometry rather than exact values.
- Reject an identity/state claim if equivalent raw forms collapse where the instructional contract requires preservation, response/help ownership changes on a view switch, replay cannot reproduce the relevant state, or a supposedly independent answer is exposed in the initial position, model or feedback.
- Reject a source-readiness claim if the cited API, capability bounds, accepted review or current interface does not actually support it, or if an exact arithmetic result is being used as evidence of a learner's reasoning. Mark missing or contradictory evidence explicitly and stop for any load-bearing decision or scope expansion.

If that difference matters to the recommendation, propose a small focused next check rather than declare a research winner.
