# Plan 61 — synthetic falsification cases

2026-11-03. These invented cases exercise the proposed contract in `history-design.md`; no real learner records, scoring implementation, policy simulation or educational validation is involved. “Offer” below describes a future selector invariant, not an authorized feature. First-visit and no-history practice always remain available.

| Case | Permitted descriptive summary | Forbidden inference | Future selection invariant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. No eligible data; or only an invalid/blank entry | “There's not enough recorded practice here to suggest a next step. Choose a practice.” | Unknown means weak, or an invalid input is a mathematical error. | Cold start presents authored choices without a score gate; no weak-topic assignment. |
| 2. One accepted relation after an in-app model request | “You chose a comparison correctly; you used a model in the app.” | Independent reasoning, construction competence or mastery; assistance is a defect. | Keep support available and voluntary; never fade it automatically from this result. |
| 3. Same pair repeatedly accepted, including a reversed pair | Show the observed comparison activity, marking repetition where the reviewed equivalence grouping supports it. | Multiple item IDs prove multiple independent concepts or transfer. | Do not count repeated/reversed pairs as distinct evidence of breadth; learner may still choose them. |
| 4. Several genuinely varied eligible comparisons accepted first try, no captured in-app help | “In several of your latest recorded comparison problems, you chose correctly.” Make the record's scope visible. | Stable mastery, absence of outside assistance, or skill on construction, arithmetic and number lines. | Offer a varied authored option and optional review, with choice and support retained; do not lock or declare mastery. |
| 5. One latest-recorded retry then acceptance, with earlier-recorded successes | “You tried a comparison again and got it. You can practice another comparison.” Show earlier-recorded events as past activity. | A stable weakness or an exact rate of forgetting from sequence alone. | One error cannot repeatedly drive a weakness loop; offer alternatives and allow skip. No precise elapsed-time claim without trustworthy time. |
| 6. Two people may have used the browser profile | Suppress personal-looking guidance; explain that the browser record may combine activity, with clear/no-history options. | Browser profile equals an identified learner, or its apparent pattern belongs to either person. | Do not personalize from contaminated data; do not require names or create hidden identities. |
| 7. A task definition changes from strong cue to weaker cue, while old entries lack compatible cue revision | Old entries are unavailable for current comparison summary; optionally disclose a bounded incompatible-record notice. | Old cued success demonstrates current less-cued success. | Quarantine from policy until a reviewed compatibility mapping exists; never silently strengthen interpretation. |
| 8. History is cleared or corrupt; a stale tab attempts a write | No history or neutral unavailable notice, and normal practice. Clearing removes record and auxiliary keys; malformed contents are not displayed. | History was successfully saved, restored, or belongs to the current learner. | Invalidate stale generation; prevent resurrection, duplicate submissions and lost-update claims. If atomic coordination is unavailable, suspend saving rather than silently merge. |

**Reject the preferred design** if, in case 3, repeated same/reversed items produce a “doing well across varied comparisons” claim, or if case 6 leads to a personalized review recommendation. Either result violates the proposed evidence/identity boundary even if storage and UI tests pass. The cases also cannot prove learning, accessibility, data safety in every browser, or policy effectiveness; those require the separate evidence in the design's follow-up section.
