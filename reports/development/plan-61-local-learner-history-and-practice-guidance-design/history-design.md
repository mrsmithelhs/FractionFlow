## Recommendation and decision point

Offer an **opt-in, browser-local practice record**, initially limited to symbolic comparison relation submissions from the reviewed Plan 45/49 family. Start with standalone launches as a bounded engineering recommendation, not a claim that session observations have different evidential value. Plan 60 uses the same task: excluding it would miss those comparisons, so design the observation around task identity and include session launches only in a separately reviewed source scope once lifecycle and deduplication are proven. Session navigation and unfinished work remain ephemeral. Show small, factual descriptions of latest recorded activity and a voluntary suggestion to revisit comparison after a recorded retry. Do not label mastery, infer construction skill, or use the record to select tasks. Keep normal practice available without remembering. Ask the owner to adopt this bounded contract before a separate source packet; **wait for that decision and for the observation/privacy/compatibility details below to be reviewed before source work**. No learner-use evidence supports a stronger summary or policy today.

The two owner purposes are distinct: descriptive guidance could help a learner notice what happened; a future selector could use carefully qualified observations to offer new, practiced and review material. The second is a later design and implementation gate. Neither activity count nor a completed session establishes learning, progress in understanding, or a minimum useful visit length.

## Three layers and learner wording

1. **Observation:** for an eligible, explicitly remembered comparison episode, record the first valid relation submission's classification, whether a later valid submission was accepted, relevant in-app assistance before each, task/version/support context, and whether it is a repeated item. Opening or using optional construction before a relation submission must count as a separate assistance category; if its timing cannot be established reliably, mark assistance unknown or exclude the episode. Invalid or blank input is not a wrong mathematical answer. Only normalized allowed values cross the boundary, never the submitted text.
2. **Tentative summary:** describe the compatible observed activity, preserving assistance and correction. Unknown, no eligible activity, or an untrustworthy/mixed record stays unknown. One wrong answer is an event, not a weakness; assistance is a normal option and no help request is not independent reasoning. A correct first selection with an optional model is not equivalent to one without that request, and repeated or reversed pairs are not automatically distinct evidence.
3. **Optional recommendation:** make a voluntary, non-locking invitation whose reason is the actual event; no ranking or automatic change of support.

Illustrative language, conditioned on a reliable single-user record:

- Repeated varied eligible first-attempt successes: “In several of your latest recorded comparison problems, you chose correctly. Try another comparison.” This describes selections, not mastery; only say “several” when the displayed record substantively supports it. The exact numerical display rule is a proposed implementation decision, not a research finding. Order alone cannot show whether these entries are recent in elapsed time.
- A latest-recorded incorrect first valid submission followed by correction: “You tried a comparison again and got it. You can practice another comparison.” Do not say “you struggle with fractions.”
- Sparse, conflicting or absent data: “There's not enough recorded practice here to suggest a next step. Choose a practice.” If assistance was used, the inspectable entry says “Help used in the app”; it does not downgrade the learner. Entries may be shown in stored order as past activity, but should not be framed as current ability or assigned an age. With mixed/unknown ownership, suppress personal-looking guidance.

Avoid per-episode explanation paragraphs, global scores, streaks, permanent labels and a dashboard replacing practice. A useful initial release can report activity accurately without asserting “doing well” as a stable skill. Candidate counts and recency cutoffs require owner review and learner testing; until justified, show individual latest-recorded events and use only the narrow event-based suggestion above. No numeric mastery threshold is recommended.

## Local mode, data and failures

Compare (A) no durable record, (B) explicit opt-in local record, and (C) default-on local record with opt-out. A protects shared-device privacy but cannot support continuity; C risks surprise and mixed-user contamination. Recommend B: explain before enabling that this browser profile may be shared, activity is local and may disappear, and that users can practice without remembering. Offer a visible **View/clear practice record** and **Stop remembering**; stopping deletes nothing silently, clearly offer clearing, and state whether the existing record remains until cleared. A shared profile is not an identity. The app cannot detect who used it: a proposed choice asking whether the record is appropriate to use before personal-looking guidance, with “not sure” suppressing guidance, needs usability review and cannot verify ownership. If another person might have used it, offer practice without remembering or clear and start a new record; never infer or create a person, name or account. Opt-in is a choice, not a claim of legal consent sufficiency.

Proposed minimal bounded fields: schema/contract version; registered item and task/definition/recipe revisions plus a reviewed equivalence/variation group; comparison responsibility and support/cue revision; first valid outcome (`accepted`/`retry`); eventual acceptance or unresolved; assistance before first and before acceptance as bounded in-app categories including unknown; coarse sequence/visit grouping for ordering and deduplication; record generation and opaque per-episode deduplication token. Each exists respectively for reading, compatibility, interpretation, outcome, assistance, ordering or duplicate protection. No raw input, exact answer, name, network identifier, full replay or telemetry. Sequence and random token are local technical keys, not learner identity. Avoid exact timestamps: a bounded relative visit order is sufficient for activity display; it cannot establish elapsed days. Do not promise time-based review until a separately justified coarse time design handles clock changes.

Proposed retention: retain at most the latest 30 eligible episode records, FIFO within a record generation; this is a privacy/storage cap hypothesis, not an educational threshold. Delete on clear, including auxiliary keys; explain browser clearing/private browsing can also erase it. Bound all string/enumerated values and reject unexpected keys. A storage adapter should validate before reading/writing, safely discard or quarantine unreadable/incompatible entries, and present a neutral unavailable notice without blocking practice. Quarantine must be bounded and clearable; no raw corrupt payload needs retention. Quota/disabled storage or private mode may lose history; never promise durability. Reload never resumes unfinished work.

For cross-tab safety, a later implementation must choose and test an explicit atomic concurrency mechanism available in supported browsers. A plain localStorage read-modify-write is not atomic. If coordination is unavailable or fails, suspend history writes and offer no-history practice rather than silently merging, resurrecting a cleared generation, or claiming a save. Clear must invalidate stale writers; duplicate submission/replay must not duplicate an episode record. Clock rollback/absence cannot create false recency, and a failed write must not claim success. These are design requirements, not demonstrated current capabilities.

## Identity, ownership and compatibility

Exact math and classification remain in math/interaction. A future small app-owned history adapter could receive validated, bounded observations from the episode/controller after a meaningful transition, then produce a separate descriptive projection; renderers only display it. It cannot recalculate fraction truth or infer skill. Admission must exclude replay, duplicate clicks, invalid or stale submissions and technical failures. A valid incorrect mathematical response is an eligible retry; an episode exited after such a response may retain an unresolved outcome, while a launch with no valid response records nothing. Eventual success is not required. Restart starts a fresh episode and must not duplicate the prior record; current code does not yet provide a proven durable emission contract. Contract tests must cover both standalone and any separately authorized session path; Plan 60 is not altered by this report.

Preserve entries only when a reviewed compatibility mapping confirms task responsibility, item/variation identity, definition, support and cue meaning remain equivalent. Quarantine incompatible or unknown revisions from summaries; discard under the bounded retention/clear policy. A changed cue cannot silently upgrade old success to less-assisted evidence. Version the normalization and summary rules as well as the task/context if reproducibility is claimed; a future selector must additionally version its policy and eligible catalog. If those versions or inputs are missing, say a past recommendation cannot be reproduced rather than reconstructing certainty. An item ID alone does not establish independent variation; reversed comparison pairs illustrate why.

## Future selection direction — separate gate

Compare (1) fixed authored sequence plus learner-selected optional review, and (2) deterministic, explainable history-informed offer from a small authored eligible set. Recommend starting with (1) while designing (2) separately. A later selector may propose an introduction, a practice opportunity or review of earlier successful work, but no fixed ratio, required session length, automatic fading or score gate is justified. It must explain the offer, allow choosing another task, skip/exit without penalty, honor prerequisites as authored assumptions rather than inferred mastery, and return support when demand changes. Unknown stays eligible for exploration, not weakness; a single error must not trigger a self-reinforcing loop. Avoid treating support, denominator size and competence as one scale; diversify genuinely distinct items without assuming changed numbers prove transfer. Finite sessions remain useful even if no history exists.

A later adaptive packet needs a reviewed observation and compatibility contract, reasons for each offer, reproducible policy/version and catalog, synthetic adversarial tests, failure/clear behavior, and separately authorized learner-use observation of comprehension, choice, fatigue, shared devices and fairness of suggestions. `docs/development/phase-3-follow-up-obligations.md` specifically keeps complexity, representation and support independent, requires evidence of relevant independent performance and transfer before fading claims, and retains later gates for adaptive practice. Actual educational effectiveness needs appropriate independent outcomes; synthetic histories or browser walks cannot establish it. Do not infer Phase 8 completion.

## Evidence used and limits

Existing project research (`reports/orchestration/purposeful-episode-task-variation-research.md`) was inspected first. Four original/authoritative sources were checked, within the approved 3–6 total budget:

1. [National Research Council, *Knowing What Students Know* (2001), executive summary](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/10019/chapter/2): assessment requires connected cognition, observation and interpretation; estimates are uncertain. A broad assessment framework, not a validated individual scoring model for this app.
2. [Hattie and Timperley, *The Power of Feedback* (2007)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.3102/003465430298487), original article text also inspected via [full copy](https://open-publishing.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Hattie-PowerFeedback-2007.pdf): feedback's content and level matter; task feedback may not generalize. Synthesis across educational contexts, not a trial of our proposed summary text.
3. [Rohrer et al., randomized interleaved mathematics practice trial, author manuscript](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED595322.pdf): 54 seventh-grade classes, periodic classroom assignments over four months and a delayed test favored the studied interleaving. It does not validate a short fraction session, a personal selector or a fixed mixture.
4. [Nuraydin, Stricker and Schneider, fraction number-line game trial (2022)](https://www.uni-trier.de/fileadmin/fb1/prof/PSY/PAE/Team/Schneider/NuraydinEtAl2022_FractionGame.pdf): 188 grades 5–8; younger students improved on trained 0–1 estimation without demonstrated transfer to tested comparison/arithmetic; older group showed no intervention effect. Game with feedback and no direct fraction instruction differs from FractionFlow. This is a counterexample to broad inference, not proof that transfer cannot occur.

The research supports cautious hypotheses and limits, not precise recency, sufficiency, privacy retention or selection thresholds. No learner study, device/AT observation or efficacy evaluation was performed.
