## Purpose and fixed sequence

Offer a short visit to compare fraction magnitudes using existing symbolic comparison and optional help. It assumes familiarity with fractions and relation controls; it is neither an introduction nor an assessment of mastery. Recommend these four **distinct underlying pairs**, in this order, with no reversal used as a new pair. Four is a bounded design choice, not an established effective dose. Exact relations and contexts were checked with the existing math and Plan 49 context APIs.

| Position | Registered record and ordered forms | Mathematical contrast and position | Learner action and possible reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `plan45-compare-02`: 5/8 and 3/8 | Same denominator: equal-size eighths, different counts. Begin with a common unit that makes the relation visible in the written forms; this is an authored starting point, not a claim of measured ease. | Choose `>` and check. Compare the number of eighths, or request a model or help. |
| 2 | `plan45-compare-09`: 3/5 and 3/7 | Same numerator but different unit sizes. After counting same-size parts, contrast equal counts of differently sized parts; the same relation direction does not make the underlying relationship the same. | Choose `>` and check. Compare fifths with sevenths, use a common denominator, or request a model. |
| 3 | `plan45-compare-11`: 4/8 and 1/2 | Different written forms name the same amount. Following two unequal comparisons, invite attention to equivalence rather than assuming that different notation implies different magnitude. | Choose `=` and check. ## Cues and support actually available

The authored `cueId` selects a reasoning tip; it is not an automatic display of that item-specific tip. In both More (high) and Less (medium), the learner opens Help and can request reasoning or notation help, select one source model, or open the revision-2 common-denominator construction. Before acceptance the first selected model remains the only filled source model; after acceptance paired verification is shown. In visual view the model is bars; in linear view it is a linear presentation. More additionally displays a general strategy prompt within the open Help disclosure; Less does not. Neither profile changes the sequence. The notation tip describes the direction of the open side of a comparison sign. Construction lets the learner enter a common denominator and equivalent numerators, with optional denominator and renaming help; its steps are optional and do not answer the relation for the learner. These observations come from the [renderer](../../../src/render/plan45-comparison.js) and the comparison and construction contracts, not a fresh browser or device test.

| Item | Authored cue | Reasoning tip available on request in both profiles | Other available support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 02 | `compare.same-denominator` | Compare the count of equal-size parts. | The shared notation, single model and optional construction above; More also shows the general strategy prompt. |
| 09 | `compare.same-count-unit-size` | The counts match; compare one part's size. | Same shared support; More adds the general prompt. |
| 11 | `compare.equivalence` | Try simplifying both fractions. | Same shared support; construction can also support renaming. More adds the general prompt. |
| 10 | `compare.half-benchmark` | Ask whether one half separates the amounts. | Same shared support; construction offers another route. More adds the general prompt. |

The strategies are opportunities to reason, not claims that a learner uses them. More/Less, chosen model, help requests and visual/linear access are not ability labels; accepted assisted work is not evidence of independent mastery. The pair 10 benchmark cue is available on request, not proof that a learner notices the benchmark unaided.

## Proposed visit and stopping contract

The learner deliberately starts the comparison-only visit with their chosen available support. Present 02 first. They choose a relation and check; an incorrect valid response gets the existing retry feedback and remains on the same item, with existing help available. A correct response displays accepted relation and paired verification; it does not auto-advance. Deliberate Next opens 09, then 11, then 10 in that order, each as a fresh episode with its own task and help state. After 10 is accepted, show the accepted result, a modest completion acknowledgment and the existing final **Back to start** action; no fake Next. A retry, help request, support profile, visual/linear path, or remembering setting cannot alter the order, append work or prevent a normal start. No new control is recommended for the baseline.

Preserve keyboard operation, non-drag touch and linear participation through the existing response, help and navigation affordances; confirm these in a later implementation's rendered review rather than treating this paper description as verification. A learner may leave early. Following the [app-shell contract](../../../docs/design/app-shell-navigation-contract.md), leaving an unfinished composed-session episode offers Stay and Leave; Stay or Escape retains position and work, while Leave ends the ephemeral visit. Return from an accepted episode is immediate. Restart resets the current episode, not the session position. Reload or leaving loses unfinished place; a new visit starts at 02. A refused next preparation retains the current result and offers retry/return; a display failure after a committed transition exits safely without promising rollback. Focus and stale-action handling require verification in any implementation.

The accepted [Plan 57 session](../plan-57-short-composed-practice-session-design/owner-acceptance.md) and its Plan 60 implementation remain a separate comparison-and-distance experience, including the intentional 02/08 reversal. This proposed sequence neither replaces it nor implies that its manifest accepts a new order: the current manifest admission checks the exact authored manifest.

## Optional extra: defer

A possible request after the final accepted item would be a fresh `plan45-compare-01` (2/3 and 3/5), using the same revision-2 task, support and help. Its distinct close pair could offer another opportunity to compare using common-size parts. It would add a full comparison and its help workload; declining or making no request would continue to Back to start. The request would be transient, with no inferred or stored preference, and would not change the four-item baseline.

Recommend **deferring the offer**: the four fixed items already vary comparison relationships, and existing optional model, reasoning and construction help supports a learner who wants another route within an item. A fifth pair adds exposure and a decision without a demonstrated need that outweighs the added workload. It is not a retry-triggered 08/09 branch. This judgment does not establish that choice has no benefit.

If a later, separately approved evaluation investigates it, compare an offer condition with a no-offer condition while holding available content and practice exposure as comparable as possible, and separately examine the effect of the extra pair or its uptake. A voluntary choice creates self-selection, and matching exposure may itself change the meaning of choice; content differences and missing/unfinished visits remain confounds. Use separately designed immediate and later new-pair comparisons and an appropriate transfer task, alongside burden and comprehension measures. Completion or choosing extra practice is not a measure of understanding or transfer. This is a proposal only: no recruitment, instrumentation, data collection or deployment is authorized.

## Compatibility and later gate

The [Plan 63 acceptance](../plan-63-optional-local-comparison-practice-record/owner-acceptance.md) covers explicitly eligible local comparison observations, including existing session launches; it does not automatically admit this new session. A later implementation must establish session ownership and examine exact record, definition, cue, support, assistance, lifecycle, transaction and truthful coverage/copy compatibility before claiming observations are captured. Remembering off, unavailable or cleared must not affect selection or practice. No session position, learner profile or preference is proposed for storage. The record is descriptive, not a scheduler or proof of learning.

Before any implementation, the owner must adopt the exact sequence; a separate packet must gate session admission and implementation, Plan 63 compatibility, access and rendered validation. This design establishes no efficacy, retention, accessibility certification, physical-device performance, deployment or release readiness.
