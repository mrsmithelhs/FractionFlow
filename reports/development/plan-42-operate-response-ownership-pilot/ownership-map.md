# Plan 42 ownership map (Requirement 0 proposal)

Implementation baseline: `6d455e733bcbc6660f4c884c9c4ed81493884a17` (Requirement 0 approved). Stage 2 implementation candidate; parent review is pending.

| Responsibility | Current/proposed owner | Authority and boundary | Lifecycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draft values/reset | `src/app/app.js`: `operationDrafts`, capture/restore and reset sites | App owns the opaque handle-returned raw draft and when to capture/restore/reset. | Mount/remount/disposal | `src/render/beat-container.js` and `src/render/linear-path.js`: update/render/destroy; public `getResponseRegion('operate')` | Owning renderer mounts region and invalidates handles immediately before replacement and on destroy; app queries selected renderer. Null outside operate or when hidden is legitimate; a missing required mounted control throws. |

After extraction, the app contains no operation definition-id allowlists or operation input id/shape selectors in `getVisibleOperationInput()`, `captureOperationDraft()`, and `restoreOperationDraft()`. Duplicated operate construction/submit wiring leaves both renderer cases. App retains draft policy/reset, active-view choice, help state/button placement, and all focus decisions. Each renderer retains its shell, including `.recovery-feedback`. The region's scene-based form selection is the one home for current response-form knowledge; it imports no content and adds no response registry.

New coupling to review: app stores and passes back the region handle's operation-local raw draft wire value without branching on its kind; the handle validates form identity before writing. This is not a general representation contract; Plan 39 must assess preview fit separately.
