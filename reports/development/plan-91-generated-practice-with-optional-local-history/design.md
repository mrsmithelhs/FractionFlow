# Plan 91 — proposed generated practice and optional local history

**Design proposal, not implemented or owner-adopted.** Requirement 0 was approved by FF Orch 12+ at `e706700`. This report proposes a finite, generated visit linking symbolic comparison and unlike-denominator subtraction. Examples below are hand-worked policy test vectors, not a running scheduler, fit measurement, learner observation, or proof of learning. All numbers, descriptors, weights and gaps are hypotheses for a gated successor.

## Boundaries and curriculum

The goal is **compare amounts, rename to common units, and find a difference**. The first candidate universe is exactly `symbolic-fraction-comparison-vetted-set@1` (12 `plan45-compare-01`–`12` items) and `unlike-subtraction-vetted-set@2` (17 IDs in `PLAN40_SUBTRACTION_RECIPE_PRACTICE_SET_V2.itemIds`). See `src/content/data/plan45-symbolic-fraction-comparison-records.js`, `src/content/data/plan40-reusable-subtraction-recipes-records.js`, and `src/app/practice-types.js`. The set is an upper boundary, not automatic managed admission. V3, isolated or review variants and data-only holdouts are excluded. A successor must explicitly admit and measure each candidate it uses; Plan 90 admission currently covers only a subset.

The official [Common Core mathematics standards](https://corestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ADA-Compliant-Math-Standards.pdf), pp. 24, 30 and 36, connect same-numerator/denominator comparison and simple equivalence (3.NF.A.3), general equivalence and comparison including benchmarks (4.NF.A.1–2), and unlike-denominator addition/subtraction through equivalent fractions (5.NF.A.1). Grade 3 and 4 denominator limits matter: do not assign every particular item to those grades without checking its forms. The standards do not establish a prerequisite, an ordering of these exact tasks, task difficulty, or a learner's performance. Comparison can prepare attention to magnitude and common units; it is not proof that subtraction will be understood. The tasks themselves supply the separate demand evidence: comparison 02 compares 5/8 and 3/8; 09 compares 3/5 and 3/7; Plan 40 identify asks which side to rename, inspect evaluates a supplied form, and construct creates equivalent forms before subtraction. Their cues and actual response/support contracts require individual validation. Reversed equivalence pairs are not independent mathematical structures merely because their item IDs differ.

## Policy to prototype

Use a versioned catalog, descriptors and policy distinct from exact admission, exact arithmetic, support, presentation fit and observation. A candidate is considered only after association/version validation, supported task and condition, measured fit, renderer preflight and uniqueness checks. Current Plan 90 thresholds are sampled emulated-browser fit classes, not universal device guarantees; new entries need measured evidence. Unknown fit fails closed. Start remains a direct launch; no required history or planning menu. Use the existing task-local help, Retry, deliberate Next, finish and exit controls.

For an initial bounded test catalog define roles C (compare), I (identify/rename), optional S (inspect/repair), T (construct equivalents and subtract). C and I connect comparison of fraction magnitude to common units; T supplies the difference. S introduces a different learner decision and is optional, not a claim that inspection is a prerequisite. The example catalog, in order within each role, is:

| Role | Candidate 0 | Candidate 1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C | `plan45-compare-02`: 5/8 > 3/8, same unit | `plan45-compare-09`: 3/5 > 3/7, same count, different unit |
| I | `plan40-identify-one-half-one-eighth-8`: rename left, 1/2 to 4/8 | `plan76-identify-seven-eighths-one-fourth-8`: rename right, 1/4 to 2/8 |
| S | `plan40-inspect-two-thirds-one-fourth-12`: inspect supplied forms | `plan76-inspect-three-fourths-one-fifth-20`: inspect supplied forms |
| T | `plan40-construct-half-fifth-10`: 1/2 − 1/5 | `plan76-construct-two-thirds-one-fifth-15`: 2/3 − 1/5 |

This is a proposed test subset of the named public pools, not a claim that the additional Plan 76 candidates already pass managed admission or fit. Expand only through explicit descriptors and measured admission. Each exact association must include set, item, authoring, fixture, definition, recipe, task-data and stimulus revisions where applicable. All source forms, offered forms, support and completion semantics must be verified, not inferred from an ID.

For reproducible hand-worked vectors use `catalog=test-CIST@1`, `policy=role-bits@1`, `descriptor=demands@1`, unsigned seed `s`, the same declared all-eligible fit/preflight snapshot, and an immutable history snapshot. C, I and T are required; include S when bit 4 of `s` is 1. For each included role choose the highest applicable priority below; ties use its own seed bit as candidate index: C bit 0, I bit 1, S bit 2, T bit 3 (bits numbered from zero). Try the other candidate if ineligible or already selected. Thus roles and optional inclusion do not share one parity switch. These bits specify a reproducible demonstration, not a claim of randomness quality; a successor should use a versioned independent per-role derivation or tested equivalent and evaluate the distribution for real seed sources. Test many seeds and an actual implementation before concluding adequate variety. A counterexample check fails any implementation that maps all seeds to only two coupled itineraries, even if their identities differ. Admission, versioned snapshots and history must be included in a local diagnostic witness sufficient to replay; do not transmit or persist the witness as analytics.

For candidate ranking, in order: (1) candidates in an underexposed family or demand group; among equally underexplored candidates, favor a proposed higher-demand variant when there is stronger evidence on a separately described easier variant of that family; (2) a tentative return to a comparable group after 2–6 intervening eligible exposures where evidence is mixed or thin, (3) an occasional older well-supported review, preferring the oldest eligible group with at least seven intervening exposures, at a declared one-in-four eligible-visit opportunity, provided it does not displace a required goal role, (4) the seed tie-break. The proposed expanded schema stores an idempotent local visit-start event with its visit ID when the first actual task exposure commits; this is new persistent data requiring expanded consent. The review opportunity uses the ordinal of that event modulo four, only within a continuously covered, retained epoch. If that count is unavailable or incomplete, no review opportunity is asserted. Use these as lexicographic priorities; no claim is made that these numbers are optimal. At the same priority, penalize the exact identity seen most recently; never count reversed presentations of the same mathematical pair as independent evidence. A family with no exposure is underexposed; within an exposed family an unobserved demand group is underexplored. Reserve both families' required roles, so coverage never silently replaces the goal.

Define comparable groups by versioned task/skill and demand descriptors, not just a family label. The same-unit C02 group differs from same-count C09; the latter is a proposed different demand, not empirically proven harder. A tentative stronger-evidence flag requires first-response correctness without recorded assistance on at least two distinct non-equivalent problem structures within the same group; one response, unknown assistance, recovery after error, or conflicting evidence is insufficient. A new harder-demand candidate may be prioritized for exploration after stronger easier evidence, but is never inferred successful from it. First-response error followed by eventual success is recovery; assisted success is separately reported; neither is equivalent to unaided first success. No grades, mastery labels, automatic support fading or individualized efficacy claims.

On poor fit, retry same role with an admitted candidate. If S cannot fit, omit it; if C or I cannot fit, use an explicitly validated alternate coherent C→T or I→T visit only when that connection and both entries pass admission. If T cannot fit, a validated C→I visit is a shorter practice in comparison and renaming, not a completed difference goal. If no coherent pair is possible, launch one independently eligible public candidate as a clearly shorter visit; if none, present a focused accessible unavailable notice. Record the reason in the witness. Before Next, remeasure and revalidate only the unstarted suffix and recalculate it if needed; do not replace the active task, identity, draft or help state. Prepare/commit atomically and retain the current task on failures and stale transitions. Preserve the existing standalone, fixed-session and historical replay routes.

## Observation, consent and reset proposal

Existing Plan 63 consent covers its bounded comparison record, not expanded collection or scheduling use. Before any expanded collection or history-informed selection, obtain a separate explicit opt-in describing both purposes; decline, dismissal, unavailable storage or revocation leaves direct no-history practice available. Preserve the existing comparison-only control and semantics until a separately approved migration. Proposed learner wording for review: “Remember practice on this browser? Your past work can help choose future problems. You can view or clear it, or stop remembering.” Explain shared-device implications and that one browser record is only a working proxy for one learner; no account or remote transfer. An owner must adopt the actual wording, scope and control interaction.

Under expanded opt-in, an **exposure** is committed exactly once when a distinct actual episode instance is successfully installed as the active, learner-accessible task, not when catalog candidates are examined, scratch renderers mounted, selection prepared, Retry occurs, a view changes, or Next fails. For each instance retain a stable random instance ID, local record epoch, exact versioned task identity and skill/demand descriptor, and monotonic transactionally assigned exposure ordinal. Record first valid learner submission and correctness when supported, eventual accepted result, and assistance requested/used when observably supported; each has an explicit unknown state. Do not collect raw answer text. Use idempotent event IDs and a unique instance/event key; retries and view changes add no new first attempt or exposure. An accepted result after a wrong first attempt is recovery. If assistance before first response cannot be observed reliably, mark independence unknown, not unaided. Partial or abandoned work retains exposure with missing outcomes unknown.

Coverage counts installed instances by family/group, distinct identities and distinct mathematical structures separately; it is not a proficiency score. **Recency unit:** the count of successfully committed expanded-record exposures of *other episode instances* between the relevant exposure and the current selection, across the two instrumented families. Same-instance retries and view changes do not advance it; an intervening repeated item is still an exposure but not independent skill evidence. If an unrecorded interval, capped/evicted window, another unsupported route or missing ordinal prevents an exact count, recency is unknown rather than an assumed zero or elapsed time. Instrumented transitions must mark an unsupported-route discontinuity; because an old app or unobserved tab may not do so, an exact continuous interval requires a demonstrable version and coverage fence. Without it, suppress recency priority. A bounded retained window must carry an explicit coverage boundary; missing old entries cannot become evidence of no exposure. Historic comparison-only sequence values never count all practice or elapsed time.

Legacy comparison-only rows may inform only their exact admitted comparison task and fields that the old schema actually observed, with original revisions and unknowns retained; they do not establish subtraction coverage, unrecorded exposure, all-problem spacing, or an unaided first attempt when not observed. Do not silently upgrade old consent to the expanded contract. Detail compatible migration and erasure before implementation; incompatible or ambiguous rows remain inspectable or are excluded, never fabricated into complete new events.

Proposed robust storage boundary: transactional local record with a persisted generation/epoch, all expanded events and evidence invalidation in the same transactional store. Today's comparison record already uses IndexedDB transactions and generation fencing for its own writes; the successor must extend or safely migrate that boundary, not assume its existing checks cover scheduler selection. Each writer and history-aware selection reads the current epoch inside its transaction; Clear atomically increments it and deletes old events/derived evidence, and no stale-epoch write can commit. Cached snapshots and prepared future choices carry the epoch; validate at installation and Next, including across tabs, and replan future work from fresh or no-history inputs after a mismatch. A visible current problem and draft remain intact. Example race: tab A prepares a completion at epoch 7; tab B clears and commits epoch 8; A's delayed transaction checks epoch 8 and rejects the epoch-7 write; a prepared epoch-7 next choice is discarded, and the next Start reads epoch 8 with no old evidence. If A commits before B's transaction, B deletes that write. Notifications improve responsiveness but the transactional epoch check is authoritative. If the backend cannot guarantee this ordering, do not claim Clear succeeded or use stale history; disable record-based selection and show a recoverable notice. Design and test migration from existing storage, including old tabs and deletion of legacy data, before promising complete erasure: a new-store epoch cannot itself fence an unmodified legacy writer. Clear success requires verified legacy cleanup/fencing; if that cannot be achieved, surface the limitation and hold implementation for a decision. Preserve the existing meaning of the remembering control after Clear; Clear is not itself a switch-off. A later Start uses a fresh record; remembering off or storage failure uses the same useful fresh/no-history policy without writing.

## Test vectors and reasons

All examples below assume the test catalog, admitted candidates and fit described above; histories are synthetic. `C0/C1`, `I0/I1`, `S0/S1`, `T0/T1` refer to the table, not new identities. A row's history snapshot is frozen at Start; it is updated only by explicitly described subsequent events. Candidate priority is applied before the seed tie-break. Equality of seeds does not mean equality of visit when history differs.

| Scenario | Seed and selection | Why / learner decisions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh, remembering on | 0 → C0, I0, T0 | Tie rotation 0; choose `>` for 5/8 vs 3/8, rename 1/2 to 4/8, then construct tenths and find 3/10. |
| Fresh, remembering on | 31 → C1, I1, S1, T1 | Each role bit is 1 and S is included; choose `>` for 3/5 vs 3/7, rename 1/4 to 2/8, repair the supplied 5/20 for 1/5 to 4/20, then construct fifteenths and find 7/15. |
| Fresh, remembering on | 5 → C1, I0, T0 | C bit 1, I and T bits 0, optional bit 0: choose `>` for 3/5 vs 3/7, rename 1/2 to 4/8, construct tenths and find 3/10. This differs from both other fresh examples in actual choices, and differs from seed 31 in role mix. |
| Same seed 0, sparse | C1, I0, T0 | C0's group has a prior exposure; C1's group is unexposed, so coverage outranks tie rotation. Other roles remain tied. One observation is not strength evidence. Compare directly to fresh seed 0. |
| Stronger easy, harder unknown | 0 → C0, I0, T1 | Two distinct unassisted first successes on nested identify tasks `plan40-identify-one-half-one-eighth-8` and `plan40-identify-five-sixths-one-third-6` support a tentative nested-identify flag; the latter is most recent. Both construct groups are unexposed; explore the provisionally higher-demand fifteenths candidate T1 ahead of tenths T0. The recipes differ, so this is a testable exploration hypothesis, not a prediction of success or proof that T1 is harder. C and I otherwise tie to index 0. If observations are reversed copies or assistance is unknown, no stronger flag is set. |
| Recent mixed versus older review | 0 → C0, I0, T0 | Both identify-side groups have coverage. Left-side I0's group has mixed evidence and its latest exposure has three intervening recorded exposures. Right-side I1's group is well supported by distinct `plan76-identify-seven-eighths-one-fourth-8` and `plan40-identify-five-sixths-one-third-6`, and its latest exposure has eight intervening exposures. Even on a review opportunity, the recent mixed return outranks the older review; I0 wins despite exact-identity recency. C and T tie at index 0. |
| Older review gets an opportunity | 0 → C0, I1, T0 | Same covered identify groups, but there is no longer a mixed/thin group within the 2–6 exposure return window, and the persisted visit ordinal is a review opportunity. Right-side I1's group is well supported and older by eight exposures, so I1 is selected over seed-preferred I0; its last exact identity was the distinct five-sixths item. If continuous coverage or the visit ordinal is unknown, do not assert this opportunity. |
| Remembering off | 0 → C0, I0, T0 | Same fresh policy; no record read or written. Storage failure follows the same selection and reports any failed persistence separately. |
| Clear then Start | 0 → C0, I0, T0 | Epoch advanced and old cache/pending choices fenced; current draft survives Clear, while the later Start has the fresh snapshot. |

These examples show manually evaluated outcomes, not an executed multi-seed test. As implementation acceptance, run a broader multiple-seed check against a reference selector, assert exact distinct identity and role-mix sets, validate all fractions and actual offered choices, and hold if candidate admission or fit narrows variety to fixed patterns. Apply same-seed comparisons with all other inputs fixed. The history examples describe prioritization for the C role; they do not claim comparisons alone determine subtraction support.
