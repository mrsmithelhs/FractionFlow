## Recommendation and choice

Recommend one **directed movement endpoint task** on a [0,1] line. Given a start and an addition or subtraction amount, the learner places and explicitly checks the endpoint. The learner must interpret operation order and direction and count a distance in the same whole; the endpoint is not supplied. This gives a line an operation meaning that location, collection and Plan 51's unsigned distance do not require. It is a design judgment about the most consequential next gap, not a claim of superior learning outcomes. Using both directions within the same small common-unit task is one responsibility, not two task families. The six-record pilot below is a recommendation, not admitted production content.

The other candidate is a single bar/line switch while a learner is partway through Plan 49 equivalent-form construction: preserve entered target denominator, raw renaming drafts, accepted forms and help provenance across a named switch. It could test continuity during reasoning, but the existing optional comparison help is symbolic construction around one chosen source model; a line rendering that faithfully supports the same in-progress construction and does not reveal the relation is not established by current artifacts. It needs a separately designed interaction and anti-giveaway contract, not a cosmetic swap. Defer it rather than imply movement solves it. Consequence: Roadmap §35's before solving, during equivalence, after conversion and after resolution switches remain unproved. No requirement to implement all moments before the owner considers Phase 4 exit is inferred.

## Proposed mathematical and interaction contract

Recommend an initial six-record pilot: 1/4 + 2/4, 3/4 − 2/4, 1/6 + 3/6, 5/6 − 3/6, 2/8 + 3/8 and 7/8 − 5/8. This balances three records in each direction across fourths, sixths and eighths, including reverse movement and varied starts, without an automatic generator. These are illustrative inputs, not approved production records; the owner and a separately gated source packet must disposition and admit them. The broader mathematical ceiling is denominators 4, 6 or 8, with the same denominator within each record; it does not authorize the entire domain. Start, distance and resulting endpoint are all strictly between 0 and 1; no negative result, boundary crossing or automatic admission of every combination. Zero and one are visible benchmarks, not required answers in this initial slice. Reject an unsupported or ambiguous record before learner interaction. Do not infer correctness or content capability from viewport size; if the active presentation cannot faithfully offer the selected record, refuse capability truthfully and preserve work rather than misplace points. A later source packet must verify whether its actual access path can admit the proposed records and reduce the set if not.

For `a + b`, show a fixed supplied start `a`, the symbolic expression, and a line with equal partitions; the learner infers rightward movement of length `b` and selects the endpoint. For `a − b`, use the supplied first operand as start and infer leftward movement of positive length `b`. The whole is the entire 0–1 interval; each step on a denominator-d lattice is 1/d of that same whole. `b` is a magnitude, not a negative fraction; the operator supplies direction. Positions `i/d` map to `x0 + (i/d)(x1−x0)`; a length of b/d occupies b equal intervals, measured from the fixed start, not from zero. Exact-core comparison of a selected candidate to the derived result decides correctness, independent of CSS pixels. Labels, ticks and accessible choices must describe the same scale; no candidate is visually privileged as the answer.

The start and expression are supplied. The chosen endpoint is tentative until Check; incorrect feedback retains it for revision; accepted endpoint is learner-established. Prior to acceptance, no completed movement arrow, endpoint marker, numeric result or aligned model may disclose the solution; show the start only, with a distinct tentative marker when chosen. A static directed interval can verify after acceptance; animation is optional presentation and must not replace the decision. Requested help may state “Start at the first fraction. Add moves right; subtract moves left. Count equal parts.” It records assistance and does not mark or enumerate the destination. A line with all exact choices may offer different cues from the visual path; it must not claim identical reasoning or conceal those differences.

`evidence/exact-examples.mjs` invokes the existing `createFraction`, `addFractions`, `subtractFractions`, `compareFractions` and `equalFractions`; its six results are 1/4 + 2/4 → 3/4, 3/4 − 2/4 → 1/4, 1/6 + 3/6 → 4/6, 5/6 − 3/6 → 2/6, 2/8 + 3/8 → 5/8 and 7/8 − 5/8 → 2/8. It checks strict proper bounds, sign/order and interval round trips using exact APIs. These illustrative cases are not a production batch, rendered geometry, independent admission oracle or proof of learning.

## Two journeys and access

1. **Addition and correction:** “Start at 1/4. Add 2/4. Place the end.” The learner chooses 2/4 and Check. Connected feedback: “Check the direction and number of equal parts.” The tentative marker stays editable; revising clears stale feedback. The learner chooses 3/4 and checks. The accepted point and a rightward two-quarter interval appear, with “That's right.” Shared Next is immediately available. The expression and start were supplied; the endpoint was established by the learner.
2. **Subtraction and help:** “Start at 5/6. Subtract 3/6. Place the end.” The learner chooses 4/6 and checks; the same concise retry remains associated with the placement. They request Help and hear/read the neutral direction/count cue above; the request is recorded, the 4/6 draft remains, and no endpoint is marked. They revise to 2/6, check, and see a leftward three-sixth interval only after acceptance. Close Help returns focus to its trigger. Assistance remains in provenance; success does not become independent mastery evidence.

Pointer or touch may tap a candidate or use an optional drag, but no drag is required; keyboard uses explicit previous/next or exact-position choices and Check. An initially unplaced response is not an answer; empty Check says “Place the end first.” The linear path gives start, expression and ordered exact candidates with the same submitted mathematical endpoint, explicitly reporting its more verbal cues. Candidate choice and Check remain separate. Focus moves to one associated feedback target on submission, with correction controls still available; avoid duplicate live messages. Reduced motion immediately shows the post-acceptance static result. For this pilot recommend identical task-specific support in More and Less: both receive the same 0 and 1 benchmarks, equal partition ticks, supplied start and operation, exact endpoint choices, and the same optional neutral Help cue. Neither receives an automatic destination, completed arrow or additional answer-bearing model. Preserve the existing profile identities and shared direct progression; this is a proposed pilot effect, not a new global support policy. No new global control convention or mandatory Continue is required. Actual AT behavior and touch target usability require later observation.

## Continuity, ownership and later source slice

A task-local registered content record should carry stable set/item/definition identities, raw start and distance forms, operation and approved lattice; derive endpoint and candidate mapping from exact core rather than storing an answer. The pure episode owns selected candidate, submission revision, wrong/accepted feedback, accepted endpoint and help/retry provenance. Presentation owns transient hover, disclosure and focus; app owns mount lifecycle. Resize and visual/linear access changes preserve candidate, feedback, raw input forms, assistance and identity; after acceptance they preserve the accepted result. Restart retains the same set, record and definition identity but clears the attempt’s placement, feedback and help history and advances its attempt generation; Next deliberately changes record identity and begins fresh work. If a future representation switch is adopted, the same preservation rule must be tested at its exact moment, not assumed from this access-mode behavior. Reject stale callbacks by item identity, stage and generation; a capability refusal keeps the current episode and gives a usable explanation.

Reusable precedents, not drop-in guarantees: `src/math/fraction.js` exact arithmetic and raw forms; `src/content/plan50-number-line-collection-task.js` bounded lattice admission; `src/interaction/plan50-number-line-collection-episode.js` contextual placement/check/assistance revisions; `src/interaction/plan53-two-whole-location-scene.js` acceptance-gated verification; `src/render/number-line-locate.js` visual stepper, linear candidate controls and context-keyed preview; existing app progression transaction. Missing seams are a directed-operation content/admission contract, one endpoint response owner, a scene that carries supplied start and operator while withholding derived destination, directed post-acceptance geometry and task-specific replay identity. Existing Plan 39 preview and Plan 50 multi-placement are not themselves a movement episode. Follow the current public episode/scene routing and replay boundary, without assigning a new schema number here; `src/interaction/replay.js` currently has Plan 50 v11, Plan 51 v12 and Plan 53 v13. Refresh before dispatch and preserve all historical fixtures and identity checks.
