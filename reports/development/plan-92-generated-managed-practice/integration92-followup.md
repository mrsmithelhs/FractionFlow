## Boundary measurements and remaining range decision

`boundary92-bundle-1.json` through `boundary92-bundle-4.json` contain 352 exact-candidate standalone journeys: 11 candidates, 1024×768 at 16px root text and 768×740 at 32px, both views and supports, and four registered condition inputs. All completed, including task and operation help and wrong/correct responses. Across 3,040 measured stages there was no document-wide horizontal overflow. Each layout/bundle had 320 nested symbolic-expression flags; recorded descendant/ancestor geometry found zero descendant intersections with a clipping ancestor. These are diagnostic measurements, not exhaustive screenshot, browser-zoom, assistive-technology or learner evidence. The fixture is standalone rather than ordinary managed Start.

The existing family-fit rule requires a measured working area of at least `max(262px, 15 × root-font-size)` for subtraction, with root text 16–32px and scrollable document containers. Prior approved samples include 1093×614/16px and 650×740/32px; the 600×740/32px subtraction sample falls below that rule. The new 1024/16 and 768/32 samples add useful checks but do not establish that all intervening widths, heights, text sizes and CSS breakpoints are safe. A practical candidate for a future range is the existing working-area rule applied to these exact candidates, subject to a boundary sweep at and around its threshold, intermediate root sizes and responsive breakpoints, visual interpretation, and real managed Start/Next evidence. No range is recommended as already proven and no eligibility has been widened. Until review, only the exact previously approved full samples reach the generator; the 600/32 sample reaches its comparison-only fallback, and all other Starts use Plan 90. This substantially limits ordinary launch coverage and requires disposition before claiming full delivery.
