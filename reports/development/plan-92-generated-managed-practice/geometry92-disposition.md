## Control overlap and a separate readability finding

The initial broad rectangle heuristic found 216 apparent control intersections, all on the initial comparison stage between controls inside a **closed** help disclosure and the visible answer/Check controls. It did not account for the browser's closed-`details` rendering. `geometry92-controls.mjs` retested all three comparison candidates, visual and linear, high support: the hidden help buttons were not visible with the disclosure closed; after opening help, all visible sampled control centers were reachable after scrolling. Tab traversal from the opened help summary reached the sampled help buttons, a comparison radio option and Check in all six cases. This narrow test does not prove every pointer position, complete keyboard navigation, focus after all transitions or screen-reader output.

The screenshots and computed styles show a **separate task-critical issue** in all six comparison runs: the Check label and comparison help buttons computed to about **13.33px**, while the root text was 32px. Their text did not enlarge with the 200% root text setting. Screenshots such as `fit92-geometry-screenshots/plan45-compare-10-visual-help-open.png` show the small labels. The controls had large rectangular targets and the sampled center hit tests succeeded, but that does not resolve readable typography. Source inspection points to the comparison button rules setting minimum size without inheriting the surrounding font; a scoped repair and remeasurement require orchestration direction. Keep these three comparison candidates **ineligible at this enlarged-text condition** pending disposition and verification. No typography fix is included here.
