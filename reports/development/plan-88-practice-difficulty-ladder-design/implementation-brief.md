# Plan 88 — one successor for goal-linked managed practice

**Proposed implementation brief, pending owner adoption and a separately authorized packet.** Deliver every adopted pattern in [the revised design](ladder-design.md) end to end: M0 and M1 magnitude, U0 addition and U1 subtraction, across five public families. A first batch tests the mechanism, not the delivery endpoint. The existing two-family path is one pattern within the broader mix. The initial scope includes only the exact public candidate identities and designated substitutions in the design; 11 other standalone families, held additions and review variants are excluded unless separately authorized. Re-pin source and run packet preflight when the successor begins.

## Single-owner integration and contracts

- Terra-high lead owns synthesis and shared scheduler, family registration/admission, app, session controller, history and replay integration. Luna-medium workers may handle disjoint family evidence, task descriptors and isolated tests without delegation. Shared edits and integration are serialized. Observe current review-model, capability and consultation rules, and report requested versus observed configurations.
- **Catalog:** author an immutable, versioned template catalog with pattern/slot IDs, goals, task features, ordered candidate/substitute identities, permitted public set and revision, item/authoring/definition/recipe/task/stimulus revisions and conditions for admission. Derive and validate mathematical relationships in math/content, never renderer code. Clearly distinguish source-verified facts, ordering hypotheses, available help and presentation eligibility. No denominator score, learner ability or support-derived rank.
- **Selector:** pure versioned selection consumes the catalog, an explicit unsigned 32-bit seed and current verified eligibility. Implement the four-template cyclic policy, first/last viability, ordered intermediate omission and designated same-slot substitutions in the design. M1 additionally requires its placement slot; if neither placement candidate passes, choose another viable template. U0/U1 may omit inspection but retain identify/rename and construct/operate. Preflight the whole candidate sequence before installing the first task; if no viable template remains, one eligible record or accessible empty refusal are the only fallbacks. Fix how a seed is obtained and capture seed, policy/catalog revisions, eligibility inputs, preflight outcomes and concrete identities in ephemeral state or a test witness; identical inputs reproduce the outcome. No promise of guaranteed rotation, no stored visit counter, profile, response, history, help, retries or inferred mastery as policy input. Preserve exact identity uniqueness; allow deliberate recurring operands across distinct tasks and document their purpose.
- **Fit and changes:** publish a reviewed, conservative per-record and representation eligibility contract for available working area and text scale; unknown is ineligible. Plan 87's nine isolated review identities and synthetic managed eligibility are not a general production fit class. Explicitly resolve or exclude the Plan 40 enlarged-text overflow for affected conditions before admission. Preserve an active task, draft and Retry identity during resize; provide accessible recovery/exit and recheck only unstarted slots before Next. Use same-slot alternatives, never borrow an unrelated task to satisfy length, and end early when required, including when M1's unstarted required placement and its alternative become unavailable. Define transaction rollback and focus behavior for preparation/display failures and changed eligibility. Plan 39's placement asks for a named fraction on the line; its aligned bar appears as explanation only after correct Check.
- **Admission and replay:** current `admitMagnitudeManifest`, `prepareMagnitudeEntry` and session completion recognize only comparison and distance. Extend with reviewed family-specific associations, validation, episode construction and completion for Plan 37/39 placement, Plan 35 addition and Plan 40 subtraction; do not infer session eligibility from chooser registration. Preserve fixed magnitude/comparison manifests, existing direct standalone routes, protected episode definitions and all historical replay envelopes and revision identities. Validate old reconstruction and tamper rejection rather than weakening admission. A session reproduction witness is not a promise of persistent session resume. Check `comparison-record-controller.js` admission and generation and existing optional local history; do not widen observation coverage or use stored activity for selection. Remembering off and interrupted-session behavior remain useful and honest.
- **Learner experience:** Start directly launches the first prepared task, with no required menu; preserve task-local help, support fixed within an episode, deliberate Next, same-item Retry, accessible focus, natural end/return and unfinished-exit confirmation. No automatic fading, mastery inference, forced remedial work, new telemetry or learning study.

## Internal gates and full delivery

1. **Mechanism — orchestration approval before shared source integration.** Present versioned templates and descriptors, seed vectors, viable/fallback algorithms, exact family association and completion seams, identity and replay rules, fit measurement plan, ownership, transaction behavior and a first-batch demonstration plan. Include direct launch, active-draft preservation and fail-closed examples. If implementation exposes a material mismatch with the adopted goals, stop for owner disposition.
2. **Family admission, ordering and fit — orchestration approval across batches.** Review all five families and every candidate/substitute, not only a sample: public membership and full identities, purpose/response and help, task-feature and counterexample rationale, historical episode replay and support modes, verified actual working areas/text conditions and excluded unknowns. The first batch may establish comparison/distance, then placement and both arithmetic families must pass their own gates; the final packet still delivers all adopted patterns. Test candidate fit in both visual and linear presentation paths where supported, actual browser zoom when available and accessible controls; explicitly report what was not tested. Never broaden exposure to held/review-only records as a repair.
3. **Integration — orchestration approval.** Exercise all four complete patterns under direct Start with known seeds, cross-family Next, help and Retry, final return/reentry, no history, and the full adopted candidate and exclusion matrix. Cover normal, narrower/enlarged verified pool, missing mandatory endpoint, missing both M1 placements and selection of an alternate pattern, shorter U0/U1 without inspection, one eligible and empty pool; resize during a draft and before Next including loss of M1's unstarted placement; rollback and stale transition rejection; no repeated episode identity; old standalone, fixed-session and episode replay behavior. Record concrete seed, identities, support, dimensions, text/zoom and whether eligibility is measured or synthetic. A partial family batch is not completion.

Mounted witnesses must drive real application controls from launch through completion and include both views, keyboard/focus and representative help/error recovery for every adopted recipe. Preserve frozen legacy replay oracles and protected route evidence. CSS root enlargement is not actual browser zoom; emulated widths are not physical devices; browser checks do not establish screen-reader speech or learner outcomes. Run focused tests, applicable broader suite/build and packet lint; investigate failures and state pre-existing limitations, not only counts.

Orchestration may approve mechanism, family batches and integration and bounded technical corrections within the owner's adopted scope. Material changes to goals, families, task ordering or dose, or inclusion of new public families return to the owner. The owner retains adoption and rendered acceptance; default exposure of held content and deployment require separate approval. If fit or capacity prevents any adopted pattern from being delivered, report the gap and obtain owner disposition instead of silently shrinking scope. Commit scoped work and final progress report under repository discipline; implementers do not close their packet.
