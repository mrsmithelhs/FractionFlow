# Plan 88 — brief for one gated full-ladder successor

**Draft for owner adoption and orchestration packet creation; not implementation authorization.** The intended successor delivers the whole adopted [ladder](ladder-design.md) across both managed families, all adopted levels and the complete direct-launch-to-end journey. A first batch proves the mechanism; it is not the delivery endpoint. Pin a fresh baseline and rerun preflight and source inventory when that packet starts.

The owner may choose the broader option in the design brief before the successor scope is adopted. If adopted, the *same* successor must also deliver the approved public Plan 40 branch and any explicitly selected public Plan 82 strand; add a family-admission/progression/fit gate after the mechanism gate and before integrating each branch. Specify exact records and sequence for review at that gate, with orchestration approval within the owner's chosen bounds. Include all adopted branches in final end-to-end delivery; an unresolved material ordering, goal, or admission expansion returns to the owner. Without that choice, these standalone families are excluded from this brief's initial two-family scope. Held revisions and review variants remain excluded in either case.

## Ownership and contracts

- A Terra-high lead owns design reconciliation, shared scheduler/admission, app wiring, registration and replay integration. Disjoint Luna-medium workers may handle bounded family feature tables, fit evidence or isolated tests; workers do not delegate. Shared edits and integration are serialized under one owner. Use current repository consultation rules and report actual requested/observed configurations.
- **Content ownership:** reviewed, versioned, immutable descriptors bind complete set/item/definition/recipe/task identities and revisions to authored task features, level, pair key and candidate order. Derive exact fraction relationships in math/content and validate them against actual registered contexts; rendering never determines correctness or level. Unknown or incompatible metadata fails admission. Record eligibility, task features, support availability and presentation fit in separate contracts. No global denominator score or help-derived difficulty.
- **Policy ownership:** a pure versioned session selector consumes the adopted catalog, explicit seed and independently verified eligibility, producing concrete ordered entries under the ladder rules. Validate level coverage, no repeated identity/equivalent pair, reproducibility and rejection of held/unadmitted records. Include catalog, algorithm, seed, eligibility and concrete selected identities in an ephemeral reproduction witness. Define any algorithm or data revision precisely; never silently reinterpret an old session. No performance, help, profile or cross-visit history input.
- **Lifecycle:** Start a practice session directly preflights and installs the first eligible task. Preserve chosen support and task-local help, same-item Retry, deliberate Next, fresh state and focus, natural finish, return, and unfinished-session exit confirmation. Handle unavailable pools and presentation changes as in the design: preserve active draft, recheck unstarted suffix, deterministic same/lower-level substitutions and honest early end. An empty eligible pool must refuse accessibly without a fallback to unknown fit. Failure to prepare or commit must not corrupt the active episode.
- **Compatibility:** retain existing standalone direct routes, two fixed manifests and their historical meanings, and existing episode replay envelopes. Explicitly extend rather than weaken manifest admission; test old identity/revision reconstruction and fail closed on tampering. Decide whether any session-level serialization is needed; existing episode replay is not a claim that session resume exists. Verify `comparison-record-controller.js` admission/generation and the optional Plan 63 history boundary: do not widen observation coverage or use history for selection. Preserve remembering-off behavior and no session-resume promise.
- **Fit evidence:** establish a reviewed per-record, representation, available-work-area and text-size eligibility method for normal and narrow/enlarged conditions; define resize detection and fallback without equating CSS root enlargement with actual browser zoom. Plan 87's isolated review identities and synthetic eligibility are not a production-wide fit class. Resolve or exclude the known Plan 40/78 overflow if those families are ever brought into scope. Verify keyboard/focus and accessible notices; record actual browser, zoom, device and assistive-technology coverage separately.

## Required internal gates

1. **Mechanism gate — orchestration approval:** before shared production edits, present exact descriptors, policy and deterministic examples, candidate admission, fit and resize failure behavior, shared-file ownership, compatibility and first-batch plan. A first batch exercises at least one comparison and one distance, direct Start and fail-closed behavior. Orchestration can approve mechanism and bounded corrections within the adopted design. If actual capabilities make the adopted journey impossible, stop with an owner decision rather than silently reducing the promised ladder.
2. **Ordering and eligibility gate — orchestration approval:** inspect every adopted level and family across all intended batches, validate actual identity, task-feature annotation, support, measured fit and holds. Include counterexamples, duplicate pair handling, known capacity gaps, and normal/narrow/too-few cases; never treat successful tests as evidence of educational efficacy. Orchestration may approve subsequent batches and evidence-driven mechanical corrections within adopted scope. A material ordering/family/dose change or new default exposure requires owner disposition.
3. **Integration gate — orchestration approval:** integrate all adopted batches through direct launch, complete four-level normal journey and verified reduced-pool/empty-pool paths; validate all adopted candidates and exclusions, changed-fit recovery, support, focus, old routes and episode replay. Inspect actual artifacts and failures, run focused tests and applicable broader checks, then report evidence and limits. Complete implementation means all adopted levels and families integrated end to end, not just a pilot batch.

## Acceptance journeys and decision boundaries

Mounted witnesses must use real Start/Next/Retry/help/exit controls and show the complete normal visit, a verified narrow or enlarged-text reduced pool, zero and one eligible candidate, active draft during resize/recovery, future replacement and early finish, and return to entry. Include exact selected identities, seed, policy and fit condition. Exercise visual and linear routes where supported, keyboard/focus and legacy standalone/fixed session/replay paths. Identify synthetic eligibility as such and distinguish CSS scaling from actual zoom, physical devices, screen-reader speech and learner outcomes. Preserve exact existing replay witnesses; no rewrite of historical identities to make tests pass.

Orchestration can clear the three internal gates within the adopted scope, adjudicate bounded technical fixes and verify final evidence. The owner decides adoption, material scope changes, rendered acceptance, and separately any default exposure of held content or deployment. No automatic expansion is authorized merely because a record exists in a registered revision. Any learning study, new data collection, telemetry or learner profiling needs separate authorization. Commit scoped artifacts and final progress report per repository discipline; do not self-close the packet.
