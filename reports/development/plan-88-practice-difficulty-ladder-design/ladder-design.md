## What is actually managed

At the baseline `src/app/magnitude-session.js` selects three slots: comparison 02 or 09; comparison 10; and distance 01 or 04. The fixed comparison fallback has 02, 09, 11, 10; the other fixed magnitude session has 02, 08, 11, distance 01. Thus **symbolic comparison and fraction distance are the two current managed families**, not all types in `src/app/practice-types.js`. Existing fixed manifests, standalone chooser membership, scheduler admission, and expanded held sets are distinct. `src/app/app.js` directly launches the scheduled session and uses the fixed comparison fallback on failure. Admission currently recognizes only exact fixed manifests or the finite scheduled slot combinations; the proposed new itinerary requires a new reviewed admission contract.

## Proposed complete initial ladder

Four planned levels, one episode per level and four at most per visit. This covers both currently managed families; it is deliberately not an assertion that all individual items are globally comparable. Each row gives actual candidates, not new content. `02` means `plan45-compare-02`, etc.

| Level | Entry or change in demand | Candidate(s); variation within level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Start with comparison of parts of the same size. | 02: 5/8 vs 3/8, or 08: 3/8 vs 5/8; reverse orientation is variation, not independent breadth. |
| B | Change relationship: equal numerator with different unit sizes, or recognize equivalence. These strategies are not assumed equally difficult. | 09: 3/5 vs 3/7, or 11: 4/8 vs 1/2. |
| C | Unlike forms can be compared using a shared benchmark, without requiring construction of common-denominator forms. | 10: 3/8 vs 5/9, on opposite sides of 1/2. Singleton: no within-level alternative currently designated. |
| D | Compare two positions and additionally determine their distance; both have a common unit. | distance-01: 2/8 and 6/8, or distance-04: 5/6 and 2/6. |

The start and end are A and D when all four levels are eligible. Hold demand within a level by choosing its alternate; raise it only by moving to the next planned available level after resolution and deliberate Next. Lower only when a previously planned unstarted record becomes ineligible and a reviewed lower-level alternative is available; do not interpret the change as remediation. Retry is the current item, and Next, help, exit and the existing unfinished-exit confirmation remain the pacing controls. There is no new optional action, response-triggered switch, automatic support fading or performance-dependent selection. A session can end early when eligible records run out. No profile, stored history, retry count, correctness or help use enters selection. The fixed support configuration is retained within each episode under [Plan 56](../plan-56-task-specific-support-and-independence-design/owner-acceptance.md); a support setting is not a ladder level. Task demand, help and visual/linear fit are independent dimensions.

**Selection contract for successor:** freeze a reviewed versioned catalog of the rows above, including complete association identities, candidate order, unordered exact-value pair keys and level. Given an unsigned 32-bit session seed, choose first eligible candidate in each row after rotating that row by `(seed >>> (levelIndex - 1)) % rowLength` (one-based level index); visit levels A–D in order. Exclude repeated identity and repeated unordered exact-value pair against earlier chosen items. Capture seed, catalog/policy revisions, eligibility snapshot and the concrete selected identities in ephemeral session state for reproduction; never persist a learner profile. These shifts give seed 0: 02, 09, 10, distance-01; seed 1: 08, 09, 10, distance-01. Seed variation is a tie-breaker, not a performance inference. Unknown, held, unadmitted or unverified-for-current-presentation candidates are ineligible; no unverified fallback may bypass this rule. Existing Plan 87 draw is a separate versioned demonstration, not claimed as this selector.

Before launch, preflight every chosen episode; if a candidate fails, try the next permitted candidate in that level, then omit that level. If none remain, keep Start available and show a focused, understandable unavailable notice. On changed fit during a live episode, preserve the current draft and Retry identity; provide accessible recovery or exit rather than replacing the active task. Before Next, recheck only the unstarted suffix and keep eligible entries in their order. For an ineligible entry, try its same-level alternate, then an unused eligible candidate from the closest lower level, otherwise omit it; never duplicate identity or pair. Continue the remaining authored order, and end naturally if no suffix remains. This can lower local demand without making an ability claim; do not silently replace an item after it has started. Preflight and transaction failure leave the existing session/draft intact and provide a clear notice. All fit rules are a **proposed mechanism**: Plan 87 did not establish production managed fit, general resize replacement, browser zoom or screen-reader speech. No sample is represented as currently fit-verified.

The Plan 40 tasks differ in the decision asked: identify a side, evaluate given forms, or construct forms. In Plan 82, optional renaming help requires entering an equivalent mixed number and does not complete the subtraction. Comparison's optional model/construction help is also separate. A larger denominator is not a universal ordering: 3/8 and 5/9 straddle one-half despite unlike denominators, while equivalent 4/6 and 6/9 may require recognizing equivalence despite different numerical sizes. A benchmark strategy, a reversed pair, actual response demands, or a different support condition can change the judgment. Evidence that mounted steps or available help differ from these descriptions, a counterexample invalidating the feature classification, or appropriately gathered learner evidence that contradicts a hypothesized ordering would require revision. Existing source/tests establish identities and interactions, not how children experience difficulty.

## Full proposed journeys, not observations

1. **Normal pool, seed 0:** Start directly → 02 (same denominator) → 09 (same count, different unit) → 10 (half benchmark) → distance-01 (compare and measure) → natural Back to start. Learner may retry and request available help; neither changes the planned sequence. This assumes each record passes a future reviewed eligibility check.
2. **Narrow or enlarged text, hypothetical eligible subset:** if only 08, 11 and distance-04 pass a verified condition, Start → 08 → 11 → distance-04 → end. C is omitted; the order still has comparison before distance. No claim is made that these records actually fit that condition. If fit changes while 11 is active, preserve its draft, offer recovery, and recheck only future work before Next.
3. **Too few eligible:** with only 02, directly launch 02 and finish after it; with zero, show the focused unavailable notice without starting or substituting unverified records. A shorter session is honest, not a mastery judgment. No default legacy fallback bypasses the same eligibility and admission gate.

## Broader managed-practice option for the same successor

The complete ladder above covers the **current two-family scheduler**. It is not a full progression across all 16 standalone types. For a broader experience, the owner could include **already public Plan 40 v2 unlike-denominator subtraction** as an additional branch in the same successor, with orchestration review at a later internal family-admission gate. It offers a meaningful bridge from comparing amounts to working with equivalent forms and a common unit, then subtracting; that is a related but new arithmetic goal, not simply a harder comparison. Its 17 ordinary chooser records offer identify, inspect/repair and construct recipes to review for a coherent progression. Choose a reviewed subset and sequence from that v2 membership, not its held v3 additions, Plan 40 data-only holdouts or Plan 86 review variants. It is not currently admitted to the managed controller.

This option requires a family-specific feature and sequence proposal with same-bank counterexamples, explicit complete versioned identities and session/controller/replay admission, preservation of standalone and legacy envelopes, and verified visual/linear fit including the known enlarged-text overflow. Mounted journeys must cover every adopted recipe and full-session transition, help and recovery; unknown fit remains excluded. Orchestration may approve that mechanism and each batch within a scope the owner has explicitly adopted. If a coherent or eligible sequence cannot be established, stop for owner disposition; do not substitute held records or claim the expanded branch is delivered.

**Plan 82 v1 mixed subtraction with decomposition** is a secondary candidate: its four public records create a concrete exchange-a-whole strand and permit use of existing optional renaming help, but that strand is a substantial new goal rather than a natural next step after comparison or Plan 40. Four all-exchange examples provide limited variation. Include it only if the owner wants that separate goal and its own admission, task-feature progression, help, replay and measured-fit review inside the same successor. Its 26 v2 additions remain held. Neither option supplies a finished ordering or makes a default-exposure decision; this is a scope choice, not a second complete design.
