# Plan 88 — goal-linked practice patterns and owner decision

2026-11-08 · **Revised proposal; not adopted or implemented.** Repair baseline `d35f9e79f5daf1f9ad2c91671f07eae74c129106`; initial investigation baseline `df7f64e963e2dd8f9449135411e3e8a34865e7fa`. This revision replaces the initial two-family recommendation with a broader, coherent mix behind direct Start. Authored demand ordering is a hypothesis, not measured difficulty or mastery. A single successor should deliver all adopted patterns; the [implementation brief](implementation-brief.md) specifies its gates.

## Current behavior and proposed scope

At the baseline `src/app/magnitude-session.js` chooses three entries: comparison 02 or 09, comparison 10, then distance 01 or 04. The separate fixed comparison and magnitude manifests are explicitly admitted, as are only the scheduled slot combinations. `src/app/app.js` starts directly and currently falls back to a fixed comparison session on failure. Only **comparison and distance** are currently managed. The standalone chooser has 16 types; registration does not authorize a type in a managed session.

Recommend five existing public families across four short, goal-linked patterns: symbolic comparison, bar-to-line placement, fraction distance, unlike-denominator addition, and unlike-denominator subtraction. The original comparison-to-distance ladder is pattern M0, not the whole experience. Placement and the two arithmetic families are currently standalone only and need explicit managed admission and lifecycle integration. A learner starts immediately, without a required pattern menu. No profile, history, answer, retry, support choice or inferred mastery chooses the pattern. The goals differ; no universal rank says that subtraction is harder than placing a point or that every item later in a visit is harder than its predecessor.

## Four proposed full visits

These are synthetic design journeys, not observed learners. Each arrow means resolve, then deliberate Next. The learner may retry the current task, use its existing help, or leave; the visit ends after its last eligible entry. Help is not a difficulty label and stays independently available under the task's support contract.

This retains the previous comparison-to-distance pattern. |
| **M1: see an amount on a line** | `plan45-compare-08` (3/8 vs 5/8) → `bridge-06-three-eighths-8` (3/8) → `plan45-compare-10` (3/8 vs 5/9) → `distance-04` (5/6 and 2/6). | Begin by comparing, place the familiar named amount on a line and see the explanatory bar after a correct Check, use a benchmark with unlike fractions, then quantify a distance. The final 1/2 and 1/5 contrasts with U0's addition of those amounts across visits, without requiring the learner to have seen it earlier. |

The old four-level M0 idea remains a local organizing pattern: same-unit comparison, a changed relation, benchmark comparison, then an additional distance response. M1 places a named fraction and then relates it to an explanatory bar before adding distance; U0/U1 change from identifying one needed conversion to evaluating and constructing forms, but may revisit a less demanding decision for variation. If the middle inspection episode is unavailable, either U visit still shows identifying and renaming the one operand needing a shared unit, followed by constructing both equivalent forms and completing the same operation. These are task-feature descriptions, not guarantees about individual experience. Larger denominators alone do not rank tasks: 3/8 and 5/9 straddle 1/2, while 4/6 and 6/9 invite equivalence reasoning. An actual response flow contradicting these descriptions, a counterexample to the feature annotation, or appropriately collected learner evidence showing different demand would qualify or overturn an ordering.

## Deterministic selection, variation and fallback

Freeze four versioned templates in the order M0, M1, U0, U1, each with its ordered complete versioned record identities. For a fresh visit obtain an unsigned 32-bit seed independent of learner data; start at index `seed % 4` and inspect templates cyclically from there. For each, filter through explicit public/admission, support compatibility and independently verified current-presentation eligibility, then preflight candidates. Use the first viable template. Identical seed, catalog/policy revision and eligibility snapshot reproduce the same result. Different seeds select different starting templates when all are viable; a random fresh seed **does not guarantee rotation across visits**. No persistent visit counter or history is proposed. Store seed, catalog/policy revisions, eligibility snapshot and actual concrete sequence only in ephemeral session state or an explicit test witness, not a profile.

A template is viable when its first and last entries both pass admission, fit and preflight. **M1 additionally requires its placement slot**: if neither bridge 06 nor its reviewed bridge 02 substitute passes, cycle to another template. Other intermediate entries may be omitted while their retained order stays intact. Before omitting a slot or rejecting a template, try its designated same-slot substitute under the same checks. This preserves each template's defining goal and avoids combining unrelated fragments. If no full-goal template is viable, use the first independently eligible/preflighted candidate in cyclic template order as a one-item practice; if none qualifies, keep entry usable and focus a clear unavailable notice. Never bypass eligibility with the current fixed fallback. Seed 0 with full eligibility chooses M0; 1 chooses M1; 2 U0; 3 U1. For within-goal alternatives, `plan45-compare-02` and 08 may substitute for one another only in M0's first slot; they reverse the same pair and are not independent breadth. `bridge-02-fourth-4` may replace M1's bridge-06 if eligible; the rationale becomes placing a different named amount on the line. Each substitution must retain the template goal and pass the same checks. No exact episode identity repeats within a visit; deliberately related operands across different tasks are allowed and explicitly described.

**Normal:** seed 1 with all M1 records eligible gives compare 08 → bridge 06 → compare 10 → distance 04 → Back to start. **Narrow or enlarged text:** assume, solely for this hypothetical, compare 08, bridge 02 and distance 04 are verified but bridge 06 and compare 10 are not; seed 1 gives compare 08 → bridge 02 → distance 04 → end. It stays a magnitude journey. If M1's last entry is not verified, cycle to the next viable whole template; do not quietly turn it into unrelated arithmetic. **Sparse:** with only comparison 02 verified, all complete templates fail and the one-item fallback starts 02; with zero verified, show the focused unavailable notice. These are rules to implement and test, not assertions that these records fit any real viewport.

When fit changes mid-episode, keep the active identity and draft, offer accessible recovery or exit, and never silently replace the active task. Before Next, recheck the unstarted suffix, retain eligible entries in order, use only the designated same-slot substitute, and omit failed optional intermediate entries. If M1's still-unstarted required placement and its substitute become unavailable, finish early instead of presenting the remainder as a complete M1. Finish early if the rest is unavailable. If an active last goal item becomes unavailable, preserve it and offer recovery; do not introduce a different goal. A failure to prepare or commit leaves the existing state intact. Existing Retry, Next, help and exit are the pacing controls; no new action or response-triggered adaptation is proposed. Support stays fixed within an episode under [Plan 56](../plan-56-task-specific-support-and-independence-design/owner-acceptance.md). Task demand, support and screen fit remain separate.

## Decision and evidence limits

Owner decision: adopt or revise the five-family, four-pattern scope and the selection/fallback rules, including whether the related arithmetic patterns belong in direct Start. One substantial successor must deliver all adopted patterns end to end; orchestration can clear its mechanism, family admission/fit, and integration gates within adopted scope. Owner rendered acceptance, any held-content exposure and deployment remain separate decisions. This report proposes no new learner data or classroom trial.

Plan 87 demonstrated isolated fit and synthetic fallback cases, not general managed fit, automatic production resize replacement, actual browser zoom, physical devices or screen-reader speech; Plan 40 and 78 enlarged-text overflow remains open. No fresh browser or learner observation was collected here. Two Luna-medium workers investigated magnitude and common-unit examples read-only; selected settings were not independently self-reported. The lead independently checked the chooser, exact set constants and task records; an initial worker shorthand for the bar-to-line set was corrected to the actual ID above.
