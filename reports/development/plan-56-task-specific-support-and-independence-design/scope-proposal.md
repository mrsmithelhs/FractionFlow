## Questions and falsifiers

- What does each setting actually change for each example, and what remains the same? Falsifier: two settings yield the same relevant instructional scene/action, or a purported difference is only presentation, complexity, or access rather than assistance.
- What happens on a help request before and after an error, and what must the learner still decide? Falsifier: help silently changes the original task or level, supplies a claimed learner contribution, suppresses participation, or the requested path is unavailable.
- What survives retry, reset, re-entry, and replay, and what does an eligible local record actually capture? Falsifier: observed transitions contradict a claimed lifetime, assistance is lost where preservation is required, replay implies assistance that is unknown, or remembering-off practice is described as unavailable.
- Which facts can the app observe and which independence claims cannot follow? Falsifier: absence of an in-app help request is treated as proof of independent thought, a correct selection as construction skill, assisted success as independent transfer, or absent/unknown record evidence as unassisted work.
- Can one compact contract explain applicability, episode-fixed or other verified lifecycle, requested help and assistance disclosure without a misleading active choice? Falsifier: its wording promises a setting effect a task cannot deliver, changes a level silently, erases task-local help, or demands schema/record expansion to be truthful.
