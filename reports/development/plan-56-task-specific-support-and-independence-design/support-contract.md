## Finding and recommendation

Keep one episode-fixed, learner-selected starting configuration, plus separate, task-local requested help. Make the setting's applicability truthful for each task: where the two registered choices supply the same aids, do not present them as changing that task. Preserve the learner's substantive decisions and any known assistance within the attempt; explain what was observed without labeling the learner independent. Recommend a **small first source slice for Plan 53 two-whole location**, subject to owner adoption and a separately gated implementation packet: clarify that the setting has no effect on that task and avoid a misleading task-specific active choice, without removing the existing help or altering the math. The exact entry/settings presentation and shared-choice consequences need a focused source and rendered review before implementation. A shared setting may still affect other tasks; do not disable or reset it globally. This is a proposed clarification, not a claim that a new control already exists or that a state or record change is required.

Two options considered: (A) keep a global starting choice but disclose task applicability locally; (B) make task-specific support dimensions individually adjustable. A is smaller, retains the established deterministic configuration and help paths, and can honestly express no effect. B risks a settings redesign and choices learners cannot meaningfully evaluate; it is deferred. If the existing shared entry cannot honestly communicate the distinction without broad redesign, use an evidence-backed no-source disposition and return the presentation decision to the owner.

Suggested concise wording for later review, not approved UI copy: learner: **“More or less support changes some activities. Help is available in this activity.”** For a task-specific applicability surface: **“This activity is the same with either setting.”** Reviewer: **“Starting support: [choice]; this activity: [effect / same for both]. Help requested: [known event / none captured / unknown].”** Do not repeat a paragraph on every screen; place wording only where a real choice or interpretation needs it. A no-captured-help statement requires known coverage and an observation window; otherwise use unknown. Existing feedback/retry language remains governed by Plan 52, without attributing ability or giving the answer.

## What can be said about independence

At this baseline the opt-in local record admits only the specified revision-2 symbolic comparison definition, condition and two exact configurations; it captures first relation response and eventual acceptance, with `model`, `help`, and `construction` as yes/no/unknown (`src/app/comparison-record-controller.js:6`, `:14`, `:45`; `src/app/comparison-record.js:6`). A help panel open counts as help for the active record even without a separate help intent. A missing or unrecognized assistance history yields **unknown**, not no. A valid no means no covered event was captured in the eligible observation window; it says nothing about teacher, peer, notes, earlier learning, or thought. Automatic starting cues are configuration context, not necessarily requested-help events. First retry followed by help and acceptance can therefore have different first and accepted assistance. Storage off, unavailable, an ineligible task, or no admission is **no observation**, not a no-help result. Practice remains usable with remembering off.

A correct relation establishes only the recorded response on that task. It does not establish that the learner constructed equivalent fractions; constructing with a helper does not prove independent transfer. Episode provenance is not automatically persisted and a persisted record is not a profile or mastery score. Plan 63 owner acceptance explicitly limits eligibility and requires a compatibility review for new episode types or changed cues; Plan 66 owner adoption separates catalog, observations, tentative evidence, policy, selection and lifecycle. See `reports/development/plan-63-optional-local-comparison-practice-record/owner-acceptance.md` and `reports/development/plan-66-research-led-episode-scheduler-design/owner-acceptance.md` for decisions, not proofs of current source behavior.

## Worked scenarios

1. **Help after a retry:** On `plan45-compare-01`, a learner selects an incorrect relation, opens construction, requests denominator help, chooses 15 and enters equivalent forms 10/15 and 9/15, then selects `>`. The first response can be recorded as a retry with the assistance known at that time; later acceptance reflects the subsequent construction/help history. The proposed contract leaves the starting level and original pair unchanged. The record's broad construction flag is not a certification that every construction step was independently performed. Exact captured flags depend on the actions actually taken and the panel-open observation; no record exists if remembering/admission fails.
2. **Same setting effect:** On Plan 53 `five-fourths`, choosing More or Less changes stored configuration metadata but the inspected task projection and renderer offer the same initial placement, candidates and optional cue. The learner places and checks 5/4 in either case. If help is opened, the placement-help event is part of this attempt; the proposed wording says the activity is the same at either setting, rather than promising a support difference. This does not assert that other tasks or a shared entry selection are identical.

## Contract and compatibility checks for a later packet

- Freeze the chosen starting configuration for an episode; a help request records its own event, does not silently lower/raise the choice, replace the problem, or submit the mathematical response. State whether an explicitly new attempt resets the history. Preserve appropriate histories on retry and same-attempt replay, or disclose unknown where no observation exists.
- A task with identical settings must not claim a difference. If a task cannot honor a selected configuration, fail safely or honestly show it does not apply; never silently coerce it into a different task. Keep access alternatives and the participation floor available at either level.
- Check the configuration registry, task reducer, scene and renderer, UI-only help toggle, response provenance, replay admission, record controller, and session lifecycle as separate owners. No schema number, replay rewrite, global response framework, persistence expansion or automatic policy is authorized by this paper. Existing historical envelopes and Plan 63's exact eligibility are compatibility constraints.
- Falsify with: a help request lost across a retry or same-attempt reconstruction; a reset mislabeled as the same attempt; an unknown mapped to no; an initial cue omitted from an interpretation of context; help suppressed at Less; a claimed setting difference with equal task behavior; an unseen level or task change; a selected answer reported as construction or transfer. Separately check that off/failed remembering leaves practice usable.
- Follow Plan 52's accepted feedback and Plan 62's presentation conventions; future source work needs focused source tests plus rendered, keyboard, touch, reduced-motion and AT checks appropriate to its scope. None were run here.

## Future policy and owner choices

A future fading policy would need relevant repeated performance with known assistance and opportunity to respond, evidence on transfer, a way to restore support for new conceptual demands, and deterministic inspectable decisions. It also needs a separately approved observation contract and real learner evidence; no threshold, learner level, automatic fading or efficacy claim follows from the present inventory. A bounded future question is whether learners can understand the applicability wording while locating and using help without losing their mathematical responsibility; this dossier is not a learner study.

Owner decisions: (1) adopt or reject the purpose and More/Less wording and task-applicability rule; (2) approve help disclosure and retry/reset/replay lifetime distinctions; (3) choose Plan 53 as the first narrowly scoped clarification or prefer no source change; (4) adopt factual evidence terminology including unknown; (5) defer adaptation until separately gated evidence and policy work. The recommendation advances the paper contract, not Phase 6 exit or an implemented independence outcome.
