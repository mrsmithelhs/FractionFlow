# Plan 75 — Mixed-number subtraction without decomposition: task design

2026-11-07. **Recommendation for orchestration review and owner decision, not an adopted or implemented task.** Requirement 0 scope approved by FF Orch 12+ at `7232df2`. Investigation baseline `42da0062310949ba79d6932e713f353d02ede759`; recheck concurrent changes before implementation. This dossier is design reasoning and exact arithmetic evidence, not a rendered study or learner outcome.

## Recommendation and bounded batch

Recommend one final response: the learner enters the whole units and eighth pieces left, then checks both together. Both candidates below use the same original operands, unit models, optional help, input fields, feedback and settled explanation. The direct task asks the learner to decide both remaining counts. The takeaway alternative asks an additional selection: identify which starting whole units and eighth pieces to remove, before the same answer. That selection can reveal a separate mapping of the subtrahend onto the starting amount, but does not by itself establish a subtraction strategy or understanding. It adds controls and potential confusion, especially for a linear path. There is no comparative learner evidence that this added decision helps enough to justify it for the first task; a button that merely instructs the app to mark removal is an inspection trigger, not a learner selection. Plan 38's grouped button is precisely such a trigger and its distinction between triggering and supplying an answer should be retained, not relabeled as a mathematical choice.

Propose this four-item order, subject to owner adoption:

| Item | Source problem | Whole and eighth counts left | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 3 5/8 − 1 2/8 | 2, 3 | Positive remainder; packet witness |
| B | 3 3/8 − 1 3/8 | 2, 0 | Equal fractional parts; explicit zero |
| C | 2 6/8 − 1 2/8 | 1, 4 | Reducible 4/8 retained in source units |
| D | 2 1/8 − 1 1/8 | 1, 0 | Second zero case with smaller source quantities |

Admission proposed for this batch: positive whole counts, proper positive fractional parts, same denominator eight, left whole count at least right whole count and left numerator at least right numerator; register exact approved records rather than imply arbitrary inputs are already supported. The broader no-decomposition predicate need not require eight, but other denominators are outside this first batch. `3 2/8 − 1 5/8` is positive overall, but its fractional deficit requires renaming a whole; exclude it. `exact-check.mjs` independently compares the proposed counts with the existing exact mixed-number API and classifier, including this boundary. It uses a local illustrative predicate; it does not test a production content admission path. Exact core values and source notation are separate: C displays `1 4/8`, and B/D display `2`/`1` after acceptance, while the response requires explicit `0` eighths.

## Comparable response and support contract

Two initially blank fields, **Whole units left** and **Eighth pieces left**, with a visible fixed `/ 8` suffix, submit together with Check. Keep the raw draft; blank means missing, not zero. Explicit `0` is required in either field where appropriate. Propose nonnegative ASCII integer entries up to 32 characters, whole count 0–3 and eighth count 0–8; distinguish missing, malformed and out-of-range with neutral field-specific correction. Compare exact value first: unequal means “Check the amount left and try again.” Equal with eight pieces receives “Your amount is the same. Group eight pieces into a whole, then check again.” Equal with fewer than eight pieces is accepted. In B, `1` whole and `8` eighths is equal but unorganized; `2, 0` is accepted. In A no equal unorganized response is in these bounds: `1, 11` is out of range, not arithmetically wrong. Do not silently normalize entered counts or call an equal amount wrong. These limits are a proposal to validate, not existing behavior.

Edits preserve the other field and clear stale feedback; a revision-bound Check prevents stale evaluation. Retry preserves draft and help history. Help in either view may say “Think about the whole units and eighth pieces in each number. Which amount is taken away?” and show an unfilled unit divided into eighths, without totals, markings that solve the task, or prefilled answers. More/Less must truthfully disclose if they offer the same aids. Record a help request separately from panel visibility; preserve captured assistance on retry and same-attempt reconstruction, reset on a fresh attempt, and do not infer independence from absence of a captured event. This follows the adopted Plan 56 support distinctions; actual integration requires separate review.

## Takeaway alternative and information boundary

Use one grouped selection action: in the visual path the learner marks the whole units and source eighth pieces they intend to remove, then activates **Use this takeaway**; a keyboard-operable selection and a linear count-entry alternative expose equivalent choices, including errors and revisions. The learner must choose both component quantities; the app must not preselect them. Validation of the selection may prompt a neutral correction without giving counts; its exact feedback and controls need usability testing. This is one selection phase with a confirmation, not a single click or a claim that all individual selections are one physical gesture. A zero remainder still requires selecting the original fractional pieces. Once accepted, the original model may show the learner-selected units/pieces as removed and unselected starting units/pieces as remaining. This makes the answer *countable* before Check: it is supported construction, not answer withholding in the strongest sense. The linear equivalent must make the same countable information available, e.g. an ordered list of original units and pieces identified as removed or remaining, without an app-supplied numeric remainder. Avoid concealing necessary information from one path while revealing it in the other.

Before final Check, both candidates withhold numeric target totals, completed equation, grouped accepted numeral, answer-bearing labels and app-generated summary of remaining counts from visual output, accessible names and scene data. In the alternative, a visible/linear countable remainder is intentionally available after a correct learner selection; the prohibition concerns an explicit app-calculated result, not all inferable information. Invalid selection must not reveal a correct removal or remainder. In the direct candidate, show original operands and only the learner's own draft; never display an app-generated removal or remaining model before acceptance. Plan 38's automatic marked removal is a useful different comparison, but is not the meaningful selection variant and would need explicit assessment as support.

## State sketches for A and B

`W` is one equal-width whole and each bar has eight equal cells. These are textual design sketches, not screenshots; labels and shapes must carry meaning without color alone.

| State | Direct visual and linear | Takeaway visual and linear |
|---|---|---|
| Initial A/B | Show starting `W W W` and source `5/8` or `3/8`, plus the separate subtrahend `W` and `2/8` or `3/8`. Same-width units, two blank fields and Check. Linear names both original mixed numbers and units. No remaining total. | Same originals and aids; selection controls identify individual starting whole units and filled eighth cells, none preselected. Linear names the same selectable units and pieces. Final fields follow the selection phase. |
| Recovery A/B | Retain entered values, including zero; show neutral missing/invalid/unequal or equal-unorganized guidance. No computed removal or target. Linear announces the same feedback and keeps controls. | An invalid removal selection stays editable with neutral guidance; no correct marks supplied. After a valid selection, preserve the selected marks and answer draft on a wrong final Check. Linear retains equivalent item-level selections and feedback. |
| Settled A | From three whole units mark one removed and two remaining; from five filled eighths mark two removed and three remaining, with three outside the starting amount. Show `2 3/8` as app-formatted accepted result. Linear names starting, removed and remaining quantities. | Same settled account; distinguish learner-selected marks from app-formatted result and do not infer how the learner reasoned. |
| Settled B | From three whole units mark one removed and two remaining; all three starting eighths are marked removed, five cells outside start, no eighths remaining. Show `2` and state zero eighths explicitly in the linear account. | Same settled account and provenance distinction; zero is visible and spoken as no eighths remaining, not a missing model. |

Render whole and eighth units at the same measured scale across source and settled models, preserve partition boundaries and removed/outside/remaining labels, and allow vertical scrolling. At narrow sizes use a complete text count summary when a draft graphic cannot fit; never clip or silently regroup. The direct design's draft visualization, if used, represents only the learner's entered numbers and distinguishes blank, invalid and zero. Both paths preserve focus and caret, announce feedback once, return focus after Help, and offer the same mathematical decisions without drag.

## Evidence, risks and next gate

Plan 72 owner acceptance covers two fields and fixed denominator notation; Plan 74 acceptance covers one two-field response and a settled same-scale model, but for addition, not this task. Reuse their presentation and episode-owned draft patterns only after inspecting actual current source, bounds, replay and route behavior. Plan 38's subtraction model supports a grouped app-triggered removal; its operation evidence is not a learner-chosen count. Plan 56's accepted contract separates starting support, requested help and known/unknown observation. These are precedents, not proof this proposed subtraction task works.

A separately approved implementation could add a narrowly registered subtraction batch and dedicated task/episode/scene/render handling, using exact math ownership and the existing shell, input and notation primitives where contracts fit. Verify admission, source-form preservation, zero, unequal and equal-unorganized cases, help, focus, revision, withholding, view parity, retry, reconstruction, protected existing routes and replay compatibility. Do not expand the scheduler, local records or old replay meanings without separate approval. The dossier does not prescribe a replay version or claim one is available.

Later mounted checks should cover A and B at 360 × 740 with normal and 200% root text: initial, partial/explicit zero, invalid, wrong and equal-unorganized recovery, Help, and settled states; inspect widths, eighth boundaries, labels, focus, scrolling, complete accounting and overflow. Exercise keyboard and linear paths and non-drag touch emulation. Root text scaling is not browser zoom; emulation is not a physical device, screen-reader speech or learner study. Test C and D arithmetic and representative mounted journeys too.

Falsify the recommendation if learners cannot reliably connect operands and remaining counts with the direct task, or if a controlled comparison shows the selection phase improves the intended understanding enough to justify burden without undermining accessibility. Conversely reject the selection variant if it merely reveals app-calculated work, produces parity failures, or makes correct answers routine counting without a useful independent decision. A study would need comparable operands and aids, sufficient sample and measures of immediate performance and later transfer; no effect is claimed here. Owner chooses the task and batch before any implementation.
