## What is implemented

Ordinary **Start a practice session** obtains an ephemeral unsigned 32-bit seed and searches immutable catalog `goal-linked-patterns@1` with policy `cyclic-pattern-search@1`, starting at `seed % 4`. M0 compares shared units, equal numerators and around one-half, then finds distance. M1 compares, places a named fraction, compares around one-half and finds distance. U0 and U1 identify/rename, inspect, and construct/operate for addition and subtraction. The placement bar appears after correct Check. Initial endpoints and M1 placement are required; same-slot alternatives are attempted first, and inspection may be omitted from U0/U1. If no pattern is viable the first independently eligible, preflighted candidate is a single episode; an empty pool gives a focused notice. No visit counter, response, help use or local history enters selection. Seed, revisions, fit snapshot, preflight outcomes and selected exact identities are held only in the live app witness, not persisted as a profile.

Each new family entry is a frozen exact public identity with its set, item/authoring, fixture, definition, recipe, task-data and applicable stimulus revisions. Family resolvers validate the association and construct the same initial state as the existing standalone path. The managed completion seam checks the accepted terminal resolve beat for placement and arithmetic and the established comparison/distance responses for those families. No arbitrary public set membership is sufficient. Fixed manifests, standalone launches and historical replay envelopes remain available; tests compare every new family initial state to the mounted standalone route and round-trip its episode replay. The selected display condition is applied to the next episode; Retry reconstructs the active episode's condition and identity.

The measured host probe is independent of the active renderer, uses the app's working area and 68rem shell cap, and fails closed on unknown geometry or unavailable scrolling. Comparison/distance requires root text 16–32px and both host areas at least `max(262px, 8.2 × root)`; placement/addition use `max(262px, 12 × root)`; subtraction uses `max(262px, 15 × root)`. The four registered display conditions were tested at the relevant enlarged-text boundary for a demanding candidate in each new family. These are conservative classes approved by orchestration, not proof for every font, browser, physical device or viewport. The 360px/200% case remains excluded. Before installation, each candidate's scene and actual renderer are mounted in a hidden, inert scratch app with recording disabled; the scratch host is removed. A changed measurement after mount or an ambiguous active host rejects the transition. Only the unstarted suffix is rechecked; losing both required M1 placements or an unstarted U0/U1 construction endpoint ends early. Active drafts are retained on failed preparation or mount, and Retry remains available on the same identity after fit becomes poor.

The optional comparison record retains the previously eligible ordinary-Start identities 02, 09 and 10, with live admitted-session, identity, support, condition and generation checks. Managed 08 and all non-comparison families remain excluded from recording. Failed managed transitions preserve the existing record admission without duplicating a first observation. Remembering is never consulted by the selector.
