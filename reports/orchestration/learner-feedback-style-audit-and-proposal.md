## Proposed minimal house style

Use one small semantic feedback vocabulary with shared typography, spacing, palette
and focus rules, rather than one generic episode renderer or identical DOM everywhere.

| Meaning | Copy rule | Visual treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Correct/accepted | One short acknowledgment: “That's right.”; task-specific “All placed correctly.” for a collection | Compact pale-green success treatment, readable dark text, modest weight/size. Borrow existing unary accepted-header palette and spacing; do not grow a celebratory banner. |
| Incorrect/retry | Short, non-blaming next action, specific when the classification supports it | One subdued recovery treatment, using existing pale-red/dark-red palette and consistent spacing; preserve entered and established work. |
| Incomplete | Say what remains, e.g. “Place the remaining fractions.” | Neutral instructional treatment, not an error/success color. Keep useful member associations. |
| Help/information | Optional local cue, no duplicated question or transcript | Neutral/accent-soft help treatment visually distinct from evaluation. |
| Valid but unavailable / navigation refusal | Explain limitation and available action accurately | Neutral attention treatment near the relevant action; never label mathematically valid work incorrect. |

Replace a completed instruction with the success acknowledgment at task acceptance.
Keep accepted mathematics and any genuinely active next responsibility. Do not add
a success message or move focus after every small transition. No extra Continue/
Finish, automatic advancement, compulsory explanation or unprompted animation.

Text carries meaning without color/icon alone. Keep a deliberate visible focus
indicator. Each outcome should have one announcement path: avoid duplicating a
focused message, live region and separate identical heading. Preserve established
input feedback associations. Native focus behavior and actual screen-reader speech
need verification when changing announcement/focus markup; the audit proves neither.

## Plan50 and rollout recommendation

Apply the owner's explicit collection correction first: remove the completed
instruction, show “All placed correctly.” using compact success styling, remove
the long generic explanation and retain accepted labels/line/direct Next. Keep
incomplete and incorrect details/focus behavior intact. Do not suppress focus rings
to hide the rectangular focus indicator.

After owner adoption of the house contract, use a separate bounded presentation
packet for existing episode surfaces: number-line-collection.js, number-line-locate.js,
plan45-comparison.js, beat-container.js, linear-path.js, strings.js and relevant
app/styles.css and styles/render.css. Change only demonstrated mismatches. Use
small shared feedback CSS/helpers as needed, not mathematical state, lesson or
global focus refactoring. Preserve custom useful explanations and unavailable-path
semantics. One success/retry/incomplete sample per relevant surface plus targeted
focus/association checks is more useful than screenshotting every bank item.

Do not treat the earlier Plan50 Terra review as this audit, or this audit as
approval of a retroactive implementation. Broader rollout, rendered acceptance
and deployment remain separate gates.
