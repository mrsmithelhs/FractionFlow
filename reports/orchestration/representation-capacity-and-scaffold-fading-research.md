# Representation capacity and conditional scaffold fading

Date: 2026-10-10. Research and future-design direction, not an implemented policy.
Owner approved Plan 32's concrete mechanism separately. This investigation does
not change that batch, its capability bounds or source scope.

## Research disposition

Conditional reduction of guidance as relevant knowledge develops is supported.
The following primary sources were checked; none validates a combined rule that
increases fraction denominators and removes support, or a denominator ceiling.

- [Atkinson, Renkl and Merrill (2003)](https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/transitioning-from-studying-examples-to-solving-problems-effects-/): fading worked solution steps with principle-focused self-explanation improved transfer in probability learning. Populations included university students and high-school advanced-algebra students. This supports example/completion/independent progression, not a fraction-specific mastery threshold.
- [Kalyuga and Sweller (2005)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02504800): an algebra-tutor experiment adapting instruction to assessed expertise improved knowledge and cognitive efficiency relative to a yoked comparison. This supports matching guidance to relevant knowledge, not a particular FractionFlow progression rule.
- [IES fractions guide (2010)](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Docs/PracticeGuide/fractions_pg_093010.pdf), printed pp. 19–34: recommends number lines for fraction magnitude and visual explanation of computations. Its evidence rating partly extrapolates from whole-number/decimal work; no larger-denominator capacity claim follows.
- [Gunderson et al. (2019)](https://siegler.tc.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Gunderson-et-al-2019-JECP.pdf): randomized short training with 148 second/third graders supports a unidimensional representation for fraction comparison. The effective thin rectangular hybrid cautions against interpreting this as proof of lines outperforming FractionFlow's horizontal bars.
- [Nuraydin et al. (2022)](https://www.uni-trier.de/fileadmin/fb1/prof/PSY/PAE/Team/Schneider/NuraydinEtAl2022_FractionGame.pdf): randomized game study with 188 fifth–eighth graders found trained estimation gains in younger grades without transfer to the tested arithmetic/comparison tasks. Estimation success alone is insufficient evidence of arithmetic readiness.

## Design implications — inferences to investigate

Treat mathematical demand, representation and support as three separate controls.
Use task-specific capability limits: full partition counting requires readable
units, while sparse benchmarks can support magnitude work without every unit
being drawn. Pixel placement must never decide exact equivalence or correctness.
Larger denominators may increase arithmetic workload but are not a monotonic
conceptual difficulty scale. A newly introduced representation or mathematical
structure may justify restoring guidance even for a previously independent learner.

Candidate progression evidence should distinguish assisted and independent work,
cover varied relevant tasks and transfer, and preserve learner-requested help.
Do not infer a validated mastery threshold from correctness streaks, speed or
reduced help use alone. Future rules must be deterministic and inspectable and
respect static/local-only product constraints. No learner study is authorized here.

## Roadmap placement

provide the broad direction: independent scaffold dimensions, meaningful evidence
for fading, and scaffold return. The follow-up register now joins the larger-

separate mastery-policy obligation. Investigate capability during first number-
