About FractionFlow

FractionFlow connects fraction pictures with calculation. Practice adding fractions with the same or different denominators.

Choose a practice to begin. Use the picture or read the steps, enter your answers, and ask for help when you need it. Replay is available for supported changes. After completing a problem, choose Next problem when offered. Try this problem again starts the same problem fresh; Back to start returns to the practice choices.

Before practice, open the gear to choose More support or Less support. For different denominators, More support offers denominator suggestions; Less support asks you to enter one. In comparison checks, More support shows both bars; Less support describes the starting fraction in words. These choices change the support shown, not an ability score.

FractionFlow does not use accounts or send learner records to a server. You may choose to keep a small comparison practice record in this browser. Anyone using this browser may see or add to it; you can stop remembering or clear it, and browser data can be lost. Unfinished practice and your place in a session are not saved. Browsers and the website host may handle ordinary technical data when showing the site.
