## Goal, reason and disjoint scope

More/Less settings change useful aids in some accepted tasks and intentionally do
the same thing in others. Requested help now spans denominator finding, renaming,
comparison and placement. Inventory what exists and recommend one small contract
that makes these relationships understandable and keeps independence claims honest.
Begin Phase6 design without claiming its adaptive policy or outcome is delivered.

Own support dimensions, setting applicability, requested-help semantics and evidence
categories. Do not design new mathematical tasks/representations (Plans54/55), their
response flows, or session selection/order/dose (Plan57). Treat those as consumers
of a proposed contract; no one may assume this proposal adopted. No global difficulty
axis or automatic coupling of larger denominators, lower support and mastery.

## Requirement 0 — scope proposal and stop

Run preflight; propose a compact representative inventory, exact questions and
falsifiers. Stop for scope approval before dossier. Use existing artifacts first.
If a consequential design question needs research, propose at most three relevant
primary studies/authoritative guides with population/task limits and stopping rule.
Do not repeat a general fading literature survey or invent product thresholds.

## Requirement 1 — actual support and evidence inventory

Build a task-by-dimension table: setting effect, supplied aid, required learner
decision, requested help, reset lifetime, assistance recording and replay meaning.
Distinguish display condition, support dimension, task complexity and interaction
access. Mark identical More/Less behavior and unavailable dimensions truthfully;
do not force all tasks to have distinct levels or audit every historical screenshot.

Trace representative supported, unassisted-in-app and help-after-error paths.
Explain what provenance records and what it cannot establish: no help request does
not prove independent thought; correct selection does not prove construction skill;
assisted success is not independent transfer. Separate evidence categories from
inferences and policies. No student data collection or retrospective efficacy claim.

## Requirement 2 — one understandable support contract

Compare at most two small contract options, then recommend one with literal concise
learner/reviewer wording, applicability and lifecycle rules. Preserve task-local
requested help, explicit learner contributions and deterministic configuration.
Say whether settings remain episode-fixed and how requests affect assistance
history without silently changing the level or original task. Specify unsupported
setting behavior without a misleading active choice. No product-wide UI redesign.

Show two worked scenarios from existing tasks, including a help request and a
case where support settings intentionally produce the same task. Recommend one
first source slice or an evidence-backed no-source disposition, not a migration
of every task. Keep success/retry/help messaging consistent with Plan52; avoid
duplicated settings prose and UI explanations of implementation internals.

List requirements any later fading policy would need: relevant repeated performance,
known assistance, transfer, return of support for new conceptual demands and
inspectable deterministic decisions. Do not specify thresholds, implement automatic
fading, assign learner levels or infer policy validity from research alone. State
what real learner evidence is missing and a bounded future question, not a study.

## Requirement 3 — source/evidence boundary and owner choices

Identify current configuration/scene/help/provenance owners and compatibility risks.
No schema-number assignment, replay rewrite, global response framework or persistent
profile. Distinguish proposed documentation/UI clarification from state changes.
Name focused invariants and falsification cases: assistance lost on reset/replay
where preservation is required, silent level changes, assistance mistaken for
independence, suppressed help, or a setting claiming an effect it cannot deliver.

Recommend owner decisions: support purpose/labels/applicability, help lifetime and
disclosure, first task subset, evidence terminology and deferred adaptation gate.
Separate paper contract, observed current source and later rendered/AT/learner proof.
Report Phase6 readiness as a recommendation; do not declare its outcome achieved.
