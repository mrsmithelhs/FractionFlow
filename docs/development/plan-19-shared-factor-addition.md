## Goal and why this packet exists

Make a shared-factor addition problem reachable and show that unlike denominators
need not be relatively prime. Proposed fixture: `1/6 + 1/4 = 5/12`, with 12 and
24 as valid common units. This retains two meaningful conversions, a proper result,
and the current final-form interaction. It tests a new denominator relationship
without combining it with a new simplification lesson.

## Non-goals and dependencies

No subtraction, new beats, mixed numbers, adaptive fading, arbitrary generation,
session sequencing, persistence or deployment. Plan 18 is sequenced first to settle
generalized family admission and denominator-task contracts before this reuse.

## Authority and contracts

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/decision-log.md`, `docs/development/README.md`, Roadmap
§§27–31, Plan 10 reach assessment, accepted Plan 17/18 reports and the current
definition, content eligibility, scene, premise/reflection and replay contracts.
Preserve exact arithmetic, frozen schedules/support, meaningful alternate pathways,
existing identities and route assertions, beat leakage and the participation floor.

## Scope

Vetted content fixture, explicit definition admission, authored prompt/premise/reflection
data, entry registry and bounded instruction/projection/render wiring, tests and routes.
No general engine redesign; stop if shared-factor admission needs a contract change.

## Implementation Requirements

### Requirement 0 — Propose and stop

Run preflight and capture current refusal/route baseline. Propose the definition,
fixture, complete canonical/alternate paths, accepted final forms and authored
connection/reflection truth. Inspect which denominators are eligible and which
choice distractors remain renderable. Show wiring from registry through validated
content to immutable configuration and scene; identify exact write paths.
Obtain mechanism approval before source changes.

### Requirement 1 — Full vetted learner route

Register only content that completes through all applicable conditions and both
support profiles. Canonical path converts `1/6` to `2/12` and `1/4` to `3/12`;
alternate path uses `4/24` and `6/24`. Keep a valid alternate form distinct from
an incorrect conversion and from existing final-form simplification status.
Do not copy premise/reflection facts authored for different operands. Preserve
both reassuring and non-reassuring premise outcomes where that condition applies.

### Requirement 2 — Independent evidence

Full mounted browser routes cover both units, high/medium support, wrong denominator,
conversion and arithmetic recovery, help/Replay, authored reflection, retry and
return/re-entry. Verify ordinary motion, reduced motion, keyboard, non-drag touch
and linear completion. Add reciprocal observations and a seeded wrong scale-factor
or wrong premise fact that the witness rejects. Measure controls and bars at both
360px reference viewports including the 24ths path and active Replay; report
scrolling honestly. Retain all pre-existing route rows/assertions.
