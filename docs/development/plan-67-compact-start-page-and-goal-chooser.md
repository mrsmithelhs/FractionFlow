## Requirement 2 — navigation, access and secondary controls

When closed, first Tab reaches primary, next Choose, then Settings, record and
About in adopted order. When open, goals participate in normal DOM order after
Choose. Focus remains on Choose after toggle; collapse must never hide the focused
element without relocating focus deliberately. Do not preserve obsolete gear-second
assertions with positive tabindex or hidden focus stops.

Successful goal/primary launch calls the same admitted factory with original
identity/fragment and episode focus. Session starts its unchanged manifest without
a resume fragment. Back to start returns to actual entry, closes chooser and
focuses its heading. Preserve session Stay/Escape/Leave and accepted-result exceptions.
Prepare failure must not strand focus in a hidden chooser or discard the still-valid
entry; distinguish committed display failure using existing safe fallback.

Direct valid/unknown/malformed fragment behavior remains compatible with existing
admission and session exit rules. The record's Practice comparison action remains
an actual direct comparison launch and returns to the same start. Preserve record
state, pending immutable saves, opt-in/clear/stop and retired-control guards; no new
recorder coverage is implied. Settings effects/applicability remain unchanged.
Distinguish public support from inspection choices through concise presentation,
not by removing access or relabeling all settings as developer-only.

Fit title and controls without horizontal overflow at narrow/enlarged text using
natural reflow and an intact accessible title; no truncation, smaller-than-readable
type or fixed-height clipping. Preserve modest touch targets, About/credit and
ordinary vertical scrolling in expanded/large-text states. Use accepted house
styling, not a dashboard, theme redesign or explanatory heading/subheading pairs.
