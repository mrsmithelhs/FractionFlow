batch. Do not silently activate eighths/zero or broaden existing tasks. Derive

unit denominator, accepted grouping and exact value separately.

## Requirement 2 — concise mounted construction and notation

Show one short instruction using structured stacked source fraction and unit names,
e.g. “Show [5 over4] as wholes and fourths.” Two labeled native inputs or approved
equivalents: Whole units, Remaining fourths. Each explicit edit updates its own
tentative model region; retain blanks as “not entered” rather than revealing an
answer. Avoid needless repeated labels/prompts or focus loss on each keystroke.
Use a separate Check and local Help. No compulsory piece-by-piece assembly.

Every model row represents the SAME physical whole partitioned into d pieces.
Keep whole-count and source-piece regions distinct. Render up to two whole rows
plus two source-piece rows, vertically reflowing with fixed relative unit scale.
For2wholes+8fourths show all four same-scale rows; do not clip, shrink pieces,
silently regroup or display an improper remainder as a mixed numeral. If graphical
layout is unreadable, propose the adopted exact learner-count summary fallback
at the mechanism/capability gate; it describes the draft, never a computed solution.
Do not claim the model's counting benefit if the pilot actually becomes text-only.

Wrong amount: brief specific retry; equal-unorganized: neutral guidance with the
source fraction typeset vertically and a grouping question, not a false red error.
Empty/invalid input: neutral actionable guidance appropriate to the actual issue,
not always “Enter both amounts.” Revise clears stale feedback, preserves values
and assistance. Associate guidance with controls; one deliberate focus/announcement
path on Check. Visible focus, keyboard/non-drag/touch controls and linear draft
summary remain available. Do not claim AT speech without listening evidence.

Requested help may ask how many source pieces form a whole and show an unfilled
equal-unit reference. It must not supply correct whole/remainder counts. Closing
help works repeatedly; view/resize preserves raw draft, feedback, help and history.
Reset clears attempt and invalidates retired callbacks even for repeated item IDs.
More/Less is identical for this pilot; no automatic level change. Reduced motion
has equivalent static content, not essential animation.

Acceptance replaces completed instruction with house success, retains accepted
construction/source, reveals one grouped numeral and shared Next/final Back. No
extra equality paragraph, notation quiz, reflection or empty Finish. For proper
amount show fraction alone; whole-only show whole alone. Mixed numeral is a whole
adjacent to intrinsic stacked fraction, one unbroken semantic group and complete
spoken name. Do not use ambiguous inline whole-plus-slash or a plus sign as mixed
notation. Avoid duplicate accessible fragment announcements. App notation is supplied
after learner grouping; provenance must not claim independent notation production.
