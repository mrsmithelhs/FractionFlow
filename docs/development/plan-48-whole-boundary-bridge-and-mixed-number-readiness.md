## Goal and reason

Prepare the decision before mixed-number instruction rather than silently entering
Phase5 because mixed-number arithmetic APIs already exist. Assess the Roadmap37/91
obligations and recommend ONE small foundation task: a whole-boundary [0,2] bridge
or whole-plus-fraction/improper-form connection. Do not design all mixed addition,
composition, subtraction and decomposition episodes in this packet.

The current unary and collection line tasks use [0,1]; Plan17's accepted crossing-one result uses multiple
bar wholes. Neither automatically proves coherent [0,2] geometry, learner-owned
decomposition, notation or regrouping. The exact mixed-number core is existing
capability, not evidence that learners understand its transformations.

## Requirement 0 — investigation scope and falsifiers; stop

Run preflight/status. Propose a compact inventory of unit/geometry/notation,

not become an unbounded fractions curriculum review.

## Requirement 1 — honest Phase4 and §91 readiness assessment

Give a small requirement-to-evidence table: equivalence as same number across
representations, honest unit geometry, actual learner reasoning, state preservation
through representation changes, useful number-line operation meaning and uncluttered
practice. Mark each demonstrated, partially demonstrated, deferred or untested
with exact evidence and consequence. A visual/linear access toggle is not a bar/
line representation switch. A correct calculation is not learner-transfer proof.

Distinguish an engineering/design readiness recommendation from empirical learner
efficacy. Identify which prerequisites the first foundation task needs and which
later mixed-operation work needs. Recommend sequencing without declaring the phase
complete or inventing a requirement that every imaginable representation must ship.
Assess operation movement separately from the accepted symbolic/model-assisted
distance task; retain remaining representation gaps explicitly without describing
Plan51's delivered distance responsibility as absent.

## Requirement 2 — one foundation-task recommendation

Compare at most two candidates and recommend one short learner journey. A useful
starting candidate is locating or constructing an amount across one whole on [0,2]
and connecting the same quantity to a stable multi-whole bar. Choose one meaningful
learner responsibility and when the second representation verifies it; do not
reintroduce a bar endpoint already aligned with the intended answer before a
supposedly independent location question. No arbitrary preliminary Continue.

Specify the unit whole, domain, benchmarks, source amount, original form,
learner-established quantity/form and result. If the task includes a mixed form,
say what the learner establishes versus what is explanatory context. Preserve
DECISION-032's raw-form/withholding contract; do not silently convert an unestablished
improper result into a mixed answer and call it learner reasoning.

Explain actual [0,2] geometry: distance from 0 to1 is ONE whole; the domain width
represents TWO wholes. Each source bar whole must match that unit, not the entire
line width. Preserve identical unit scale across stacks, endpoints and any
connector, including exact whole-boundary cases. No viewport-derived correctness
or automatic larger-denominator admission. Recommend manageable bounds from current
capability, not unlimited number-line resolution.

Include wrong/revised response, requested help, pointer/touch and keyboard/non-drag
linear equivalence, reduced motion, More/Less, view/representation change semantics
and direct progression. Use existing stacked typography and propose a bounded
mixed-number display contract if needed; avoid ambiguous inline whole-plus-slash
notation. No long explanatory preamble, duplicated prompts or every regrouping
step packed into one episode. Show two worked journeys and compact layout sketches.
Apply Plan52's accepted feedback vocabulary in those sketches and distinguish
initial unplaced, tentative, incorrect and accepted positions; do not let a tile's
starting position or an aligned source model supply the answer before Check.

## Requirement 3 — exact examples and source seam recommendation

Check roughly 6–8 illustrative cases through existing exact APIs, covering the
chosen foundation task's proper/improper/unit-boundary/equivalent forms as
appropriate. Treat zero/whole-only remainders explicitly. Derive correspondence
and unit counts; no handwritten arithmetic oracle. Trace composition/decomposition
APIs to distinguish available truth from missing instructional meaning, without
rewriting them or asserting a new operation domain.

Recommend compact data, stable identities/raw forms, task schedule/intents,
response/help ownership, scene withholding, display/capability and replay boundaries
for ONE later source slice. Call out actual missing seams and whether a small
ownership/tooling prerequisite is justified. No general representation framework,
global focus/refactor, schema-number assignment or source implementation here.
Refresh the MAINT-42-B/C dispositions and the representation-evidence tooling
obligation against current seams; recommend a prerequisite only for a concrete
cost or defect, not as automatic permission for a broad refactor.

Map subsequent mixed foundations, addition without composition, composition,
subtraction without decomposition and decomposition only as later dependencies
and decision questions. Do not author all their flows. Disposition OQ-24 as a
recommendation for the owner, not an automatic closure. Larger denominators,
multiplication/division, adaptive fading and persistent progress remain separate.

## Requirement 4 — bounded implementation/evidence and owner choices

Provide one first-source recommendation and a minimal branch-to-evidence plan.
Separate exact/all-record checks from actual rendered/access/learner evidence.
Specify ordinary-assertion seeds for doubled/halved unit scale, whole-boundary
off-by-one, unestablished mixed-form reveal and lost state during representation
change. Annotated sketches cannot establish pixel geometry or usability.

Recommend answers to the small owner decisions: first learning purpose, what
forms may be supplied, domain/unit bounds, necessary Phase4 prerequisites and
what remains deferred. If source evidence cannot distinguish two teaching
approaches, say so and name a bounded next check; do not claim a research winner.
