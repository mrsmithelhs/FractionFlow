## Goal, adoption and sequence

Teach a distinct meaning of subtraction: the distance between two amounts, rather
than removing an amount from a starting collection. Present original ordered
fractions, let the learner establish their relation once, then give a fractional
distance. Keep the accepted comparison as context; do not ask a duplicate verbal
greater-than judgment. Optional model help supports counting honestly; final
acceptance leads directly to shared Next/final Back, without another reflection.

The owner adopted Plan47's refined task and four-record slice on 2026-11-01.

governing contract. This packet runs AFTER owner-closed Plan50, not concurrently:

## Requirement 2 — one active task and honest assistance

Initially show original stacked fractions and one relation choice plus Check,
with full accessible spoken meanings. No result, gap, paired models or chosen
answer. Pending relation uses the accepted one-source-model rule: first chosen
model stays fixed through retry/help/view/resize/remount. Keep notation/reasoning
assistance non-answer-bearing; no automatic Plan49 workspace for this task.

Correct comparison becomes inspectable established context. Replace the question
with one “How far apart are these amounts?” prompt and fraction response. Focus
the numerator. Do not also render an equivalent heading/subheading or another
comparison question. Do not show paired bars or a derived subtraction/result label
automatically at this transition.

Use one local optional help disclosure. Text cue may say “Use the greater amount
minus the smaller amount. The parts have the same size.” More may project the
brief cue; Less leaves it on request, with immutable existing support profiles.
An explicit “Show both amounts” action may reveal honest equal-whole, common-unit
bars while distance is pending. Counting their difference is assisted model
reading even without a numeric label; record it honestly. No distorted geometry
or false claim the answer remains hidden. Linear help supplies equivalent amount/
unit information with the same assistance consequence. Model help must not be
silently triggered by a wrong answer.

Wrong distance preserves the accepted relation, current fraction draft and help,
with concise associated feedback and focus on a connected current control. Do not
diagnose a misconception from one wrong response. Close/reopen/view/resize/remount
preserve useful work; retired controls cannot mutate a new stage/item. Explicit
restart, Next and return/re-entry reset through established fresh-state rules.
View/draft state stays app-local; no new navigation history or tracking feature.

After exact acceptance, retain ordered originals, accepted relation and learner's
distance form, with honest paired verification identifying the region between
amount endpoints as distance. Avoid takeaway removal hatching/wording. The same
one-whole width and subdivision scale must hold for both bars. Interval length
is not an endpoint coordinate. Use existing shared direct Next/final Back with
generation checks, deliberate focus and preserved result on navigation failure.
No extra Finish, timer, explanation response or parallel progression controller.
