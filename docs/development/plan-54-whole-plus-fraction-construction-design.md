## Goal, reason and sequence

The owner accepted this next design direction after closing Plan53 on 2026-11-02.
Plan53 asks learners to locate a supplied improper fraction, then provides the
whole/remainder verification. The next missing responsibility is for learners to
establish a whole-plus-fraction construction themselves and connect it to notation.
Recommend ONE short task, not a compulsory extension of every location episode.

Compare at most two approaches: manipulating whole/fractional units, or entering
whole and remaining-unit quantities with a meaningful model response. These are
candidates, not adopted interactions. Recommend the smallest task that retains
mathematical thinking and avoids laborious repeated clicks or redundant questions.
Do not select an approach solely because it reuses more code or looks more animated.

Dependencies establish adopted boundaries, existing two-whole geometry, typography
and feedback. This is not automatic Phase5 activation: explicitly assess remaining
Phase4/§91 prerequisites and OQ-24 questions, distinguishing this foundation from
later addition/subtraction, composition and decomposition strategies.

## Requirement 1 — compare purpose and choose one learner responsibility

Give a concise comparison of the two candidates: what the learner must determine,
what is supplied, how a wrong response reveals a task-relevant difference, help,
gesture/counting burden, accessible participation and implementation seams. State
what existing evidence cannot distinguish; do not claim a teaching winner from
engineering simplicity, animation or model consultation.

Recommend one short journey, likely supplied improper fraction -> construction ->
Check/revise -> accepted mixed-form connection -> direct Next. Refine this if the
evidence supports a better bounded responsibility. No preliminary Continue, separate
acknowledgment or empty Finish. No automatic location-plus-construction-plus-conversion

## Requirement 2 — exact mathematical and representation contract

Recommend compact initial bounds, ordinarily anchored to Plan53's [0,2] and admitted
units; any greater range needs an explicit reason and owner choice. Check roughly
6–8 illustrative cases with current exact APIs: proper contrast, interior whole,
improper amount, exact upper boundary, equivalent unreduced forms, and zero if
relevant to the selected task. Examples are not an admitted production batch.

Preserve raw inputs and exact numerical identity. Distinguish mathematically
equivalent but task-incomplete construction from a wrong amount: e.g. 0 wholes plus
5/4 is numerically 5/4, though it may not meet a stated whole-plus-proper-remainder
goal. Recommend accept/normalize/request-organization semantics explicitly; never
call valid arithmetic incorrect merely because the task asks for another grouping.
Decide how unreduced remainders such as 2/4 and 1/2 are treated, whether source units
must be retained and when help/conversion would be needed. No forced simplification
unless adopted as the actual responsibility. Explain zero/whole-only and remainder
less-than-one rules separately from the core's ability to represent transient forms.

Keep each whole the same physical unit. Distinguish counting pieces, grouping them
into a whole and changing notation. State when a line or bar appears, which one
supports thinking and which verifies; do not expose a completed grouping before
asking the learner to establish it. Avoid a new representation-switch framework.
Use exact core composition/decomposition APIs only for truth, not inferred pedagogy.

## Requirement 3 — concise interaction, help and typography design

Show two concrete worked journeys, including wrong/revised/requested-help, and
compact narrow/Chromebook sketches. Mark sketches as unmeasured. Specify initial
unanswered state, tentative manipulation/input, explicit Check, classification,
feedback, revision, acceptance and shared Next/final Back. Give literal short copy;
one instruction and one relevant feedback message, not heading/subheading repeats.
Use Plan52 success/retry/neutral-attention semantics and retain meaningful math.

Pointer/touch manipulation must have a meaningful non-drag/keyboard/linear equivalent.
Avoid laborious one-piece-at-a-time assembly without a considered alternative.
Describe selected objects, grouping boundaries, correction controls, touch spacing,
focus/associations, announcements and reduced motion; no real AT/device efficacy
claim from paper. Preserve response/help/assistance through view/resize where
applicable; define reset, stale controls and what replay must reproduce.

Help is optional, local to the responsibility and thought-provoking without doing
the whole construction. More/Less differences must change a useful aid; recommending
identical pilot support is valid if justified. Do not introduce an adaptive policy.

Propose one mixed-number typography contract: whole numeral next to an intrinsic
stacked fraction, readable grouping, coherent spoken name and no ambiguous inline
whole-plus-slash notation. Show whole-only, proper-only, one/two-digit cases and
enlarged-text wrapping expectations. Preserve the distinction between a mixed form
and an addition expression. Explain which parts can reuse current typography and
which need a small new component. No implementation or pixel-usability claim here.
