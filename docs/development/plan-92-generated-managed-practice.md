## Goal and authority

Deliver generated visits across symbolic comparison and unlike-denominator subtraction through **Start a practice session**. The scheduler chooses a compatible path and eligible problems for its roles, with or without optional local history. The result must vary the mathematical work learners do across visits while keeping the session goal coherent. Use the existing **Next problem**, Retry, help, finish and exit behaviors.

## Generator and measured admission

1. Generate from three coherent path shapes under the common goal of comparing amounts, working with common units and finding a difference. Use comparison → identify and rename → construct and subtract, comparison → inspect and keep or repair → construct and subtract, and a visit including both intermediate decisions. Use a reviewed compatibility map with mathematical reasons for transitions. Derive path and role draws separately from the fresh seed. Use bounded deterministic selection with versioned inputs and independent role choices. One seed and identical inputs must reproduce a visit. A no-history visit requires no persisted visit state or guaranteed rotation.
2. Grow beyond the eight examples. At normal fit, target at least three comparison decision structures, three identify candidates covering both left and right renaming, three construct candidates spanning distinct common-unit structures, and two inspect candidates with different offered-form judgments. Validate the target against the public records and the actual task prompts, help and responses. If the eligible pools cannot support it, report the concrete shortfall at the gate. Keep curriculum mappings, task demand, support, exact math and presentation fit separate. Verify each admitted candidate in both participation views and supported conditions, including narrow and enlarged-text cases. Unknown fit fails closed.
3. Measure **problem variety** using exact identities and canonical mathematical keys. Reversed comparison pairs collapse to the same key. Measure **decision variety** separately using path roles, comparison relation or benchmark structure, denominator relationship, which side needs renaming, and actual keep, repair or construction work. Raw operand values and record IDs do not distinguish decision signatures. Run an executable reference comparison over at least 64 documented seeds with a normally measured eligible pool. Target all three shapes and at least 12 distinct decision signatures. Report both counts and frequency distributions. Reject a selector that mostly repeats a couple of itineraries or meets the target through numeric swaps. Demonstrate sequential fresh Starts and variation under a narrower measured pool, with its actual limitations. These thresholds are engineering targets for the admission gate, not evidence of learning.
4. Keep Start direct with or without history. Preflight the whole visit and keep the active episode, draft and Retry identity through fit changes. Recheck only unstarted work. When a role cannot fit, use an admitted substitute or a reviewed coherent shorter or alternate path. Use an independently eligible single problem if necessary, or a focused unavailable notice when none fits. Preserve transactional rollback and stale-selection refusal. Keep exact arithmetic and admissibility outside the renderer.

## Optional local record and selection policy

- Obtain explicit opt-in that describes expanded observations and their use to choose practice before either occurs. Preserve the separately enabled legacy comparison record and its eligibility unless the owner approves a migration. When expanded remembering is off, the scheduler reads no history and creates no expanded observations. Keep local inspection, Clear and stop controls, shared-browser wording and a useful no-history path on storage failure.
- Record one exposure for a distinct learner-accessible installed episode, with first valid response, eventual acceptance and assistance represented separately when observable. Keep missing evidence unknown. Deduplicate retries, view switches and failed preflight. Store no raw answers. Preserve versioned identities, a bounded retention window and explicit coverage boundaries. Legacy comparison entries can only support claims within their original coverage and any newly approved use. Neither their sequence nor a gap in coverage establishes all-problem or elapsed recency.
- Use underexplored work and tentative evidence from distinct comparable problems to select among compatible candidates. Separate recent need in a skill group from recent repetition of a specific problem or decision structure. Prefer suitable less-recent candidates when remembering is on, while allowing a timely return to a skill. Give recent mixed evidence priority over older well-supported review. Derive an occasional older-review opportunity from an independent draw using the session seed, initially around one in four visits when eligible. Store no visit counter for this purpose. Preserve uncertainty and prohibit mastery labels, inferred learner grade and automatic support fading.
- Clear keeps the current episode and draft, preserves the existing remembering control semantics, and makes the next Start use a fresh record. Fence stale writes, cached evidence and prepared choices across tabs with an authoritative transactional generation. Verify both orderings of Clear and delayed activity, and supported older app tabs and legacy storage. Return for disposition if the proposed migration cannot guarantee the agreed erasure and reset.

3. **Integration.** Deliver all three path shapes and the full two-family loop. Use same-seed fresh versus history-informed examples that change a comparison and a subtraction choice. Exercise recent need versus older review, exact-item reuse versus skill return, partial/conflicting evidence, remembering off, storage failure, clear then fresh Start, and resize with current draft intact. Run focused tests, applicable full suite/build, protected browser routes, packet lint and scoped diff checks. Preserve frozen replay oracles.
4. **Owner rendered review.** Provide a repeatable local preview with seeds and synthetic histories, real mounted Start-to-finish journeys, both views and keyboard/focus evidence, consent and Clear, and evidence limits. A successful first batch is a checkpoint. Full delivery requires the complete scope and owner rendered review. Commit scoped work with `progress.md` last.
