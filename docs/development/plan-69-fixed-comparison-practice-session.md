Use existing shell conventions for early exit, Stay/Escape, focus, restart and return. An unfinished episode offers Stay/Leave on return. Stay preserves work and focus, and Leave ends the session. Assess Plan 63 compatibility explicitly for identity, cue, support, assistance, lifecycle and record admission. Demonstrate the resulting truthful coverage and preserve existing record behavior.
