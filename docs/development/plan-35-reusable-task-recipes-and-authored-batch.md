## Goal and why this packet exists

Make suitable additional problems compact data additions to proven tasks. Plan 32
adopted compact numerical authoring. Plan 34 proved two compact two-renaming tasks,
but currently admits three exact records at target 12. Plan 18 proved meaningful
side identification and one construction in both orders, but admits two records
at target 8. Those are valid bounded deliveries, not general authoring capability.

This packet removes those target/example limits for a reviewed set of compatible
proper-addition tasks. A new compatible record must not require an app/render
branch, a new reducer, model-calculated answers or another numerical micro-packet.
Recipe identity describes learner responsibilities; problem identity describes
the quantities. Preserve that distinction in authoring, state, scene and replay.

## Non-goals and dependencies

No new mathematical operation, subtraction integration, like-denominator recipe
redesign, crossing-one-whole recipe, mixed numbers, unreduced starting operands,
active simplification, unrestricted nested-unit choice, new representation,
capacity expansion, random/adaptive selection, progress tracking, study,
framework migration, plugin engine, dependency change, push or deployment.

Plan 18 supplies the accepted one-renaming task and truthful provided operand;
Plan 34 supplies construct and inspect/keep/repair; Plan 32 supplies production
compact authoring. Their dependencies already supply notation, exact math, local
help, immutable schedules and learner-controlled Next. These are real baselines,
not authorization to rewrite their historical definitions or representations.

3. **Batch.** Propose a discriminating manifest, fixed initial assignments and

5. **Routes and lifecycle.** Identify current versus historical fixtures honestly.

## Requirement 1 — Reusable task contracts

| Recipe | Eligible reviewed content | Learner responsibilities and invariants |
|---|---|---|
| Construct equivalents | Proper addition with two operands needing renaming to a supplied valid target; current relatively-prime/shared-factor relationships | Encounter, construct each required equivalent, one operation answer, finish. Target is concise provided context. No denominator-choice or extra Notice/reflection. |
| Inspect/keep/repair | Same two-renaming bounds; versioned authored candidates | Keep or change each candidate, validate exact submitted form, then add and finish. Preserve authored stimulus, actual action, confirmed/repaired/valid-unchanged classifications. No validity cue before response. |
| Identify side, then construct | Proper nested addition with exactly one operand already in the shared target unit | Encounter, ask “Which fraction needs a different denominator?”, construct only the required side, add and finish. Target unit is the already available common unit; untouched operand is provided original context. Wrong-side recovery cannot change the frozen transform side. |

For this packet, admitted operands remain reduced positive proper fractions and
the sum remains below one. Do not broaden a recipe silently to zero conversions,
crossing results or noncanonical nested targets that require two conversions.
Reduced results may remain raw accepted forms; no new simplification lesson.

Derive the required side(s), equivalents and results with exact APIs and validate
the authored target. Generalize local question/help target context without
reintroducing the target hint in the side-choice question. Do not use one
per-problem wording branch or a new learner taxonomy button for every recipe.

Preserve the operation form actually established by the learner. Provided,
constructed, confirmed and repaired forms must not collapse into one provenance
category. A valid unchanged edit is not a math error. Help preserves drafts,
remains requested and local, and never supplies/accepts the required numerator.
Keep actual registered high/medium profiles and both views; do not invent support
differences or extra questions to manufacture a condition distinction.

## Requirement 2 — Varied batch and data-only extension proof

At Requirement 0 select the smallest useful batch meeting these dimensions:

- At least three distinct target denominators represented for each recipe; vary
  scale-factor relationships rather than merely swapping numerator values.
- More than one numerical instance per recipe. Cover both operand orders for
  one-renaming content and vary which side needs work.
- Inspection stimuli include valid and false candidates, with error location and
  keep/change outcomes varied. Constant action or fixed-side guessing must not
  solve the admitted sequence.
- Include reducible and irreducible raw sums without adding simplification work.
  Stay within current declared eligibility/geometry limits; new denominator
  capacity is a separate obligation.

Reuse suitable existing content as well as compact new records. Do not duplicate
the entire bank for every recipe or mandate every possible recipe/problem pair as
a learner episode. Fixed live associations are authored and versioned; additional
compatibility pairs can remain test-only and must be labeled accordingly.

After the shared mechanism is checkpointed, add the declared hold-out compatible
records in at least two recipes using data/association changes only. Generic
validation/tests must discover and complete them without new reducer/renderer
branches or hand-authored answer assertions. Show the actual diff proving this.
If the hold-out requires new behavioral source, explain the missing generality
and repair the shared mechanism; do not call a copied special case data-driven.

Retain the accepted 13-item numerical/behavior prefix and its historical meanings.
Append a reviewed fixed batch in a new set revision unless the owner explicitly
adopts a different sequence. No automatic session length, sampler or persistence.

## Requirement 3 — Validation and honest evidence

Validate every admitted record/recipe/target under applicable registered profiles:
exact content identity, frozen schedule, local recovery, effective forms and
provenance, operation completion, reset and replay. Assert incompatible recipe
bindings, missing/extra context, malformed candidates and forged revisions fail
closed. Preserve old replay oracles and historical route behavior.

Use a small mapped browser sample spanning the genuinely distinct responsibilities,
targets/geometry, both views, actual profiles, keyboard/touch-context and reduced
motion. Deep production reach and focused unshipped sweeps are different claims.
Do not require an uninformative full Cartesian screenshot matrix. Measure at
360x740/752 and an actual Chromebook CSS viewport, including enlarged-text/zoom
where target formatting changes wrapping. Report ordinary vertical scrolling.

Reuse meaningful motion evidence for a distinct new subdivision case and unchanged
operand conservation, including direct WAAPI observation where that is the
mechanism. No-motion claims cannot come from a linear view that has no bars.
Measure viewport intersection, not just positive element boxes. Seed an incompatible
task binding or leaked answer and prove the applicable witness fails at its
intended assertion, then retain a clean receipt separately from seed receipts.

Run appropriate focused checks, full tests, both builds, full route matrix, packet
lint and diff check. Preserve accepted prior-plan screenshots when regression
runs regenerate them; store current evidence in this packet's folder.
