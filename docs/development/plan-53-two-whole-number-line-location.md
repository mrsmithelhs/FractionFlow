## Requirement 2 — short mounted task and honest geometry

One prompt, e.g. “Place this fraction on the number line.” Show the stacked source
fraction in a clearly separate starting area, not apparently located on the line.
Reuse accepted hover/grab affordances and nearest-candidate highlight: drag release
commits only a tentative candidate. Provide direct non-drag pointer/touch placement
and keyboard/linear controls with equivalent exact positions and separate Check.
No preliminary Continue or invisible preview language on the linear path. Accessible
names identify the offered position meaningfully; no per-item authored English keys.

Show fixed benchmarks 0,1,2 and equal partition ticks. More and Less use identical
default candidates/partitions in this pilot; do not add a second artificial task.
Requested help may highlight equal whole intervals and give one short useful cue,
without moving the tile, marking the target or revealing a solution bar. Correctness
and assistance remain upstream; renderer only projects validated state.

Retry is specific, concise and non-blaming; incomplete placement is neutral. Use
Plan52 treatments and one deliberate announcement/focus path, preserving input
associations and visible focus. Accepted success replaces the completed instruction
without redundant praise/context. Show accepted line point and bar verification,
then shared Next problem/final Back to start immediately. No empty Finish step.

Let x0,x2 be line endpoints and U=(x2-x0)/2. Benchmark 1 is x0+U; candidate i/d is
x0+(i/d)U. Each stacked bar whole has width U, begins at x0 and ends at x0+U.
For 1+r/d the second bar fill ends at x0+(r/d)U while the line point is U farther
right. Do not draw an endpoint-alignment connector. At 1 show exactly one filled
whole; at 2 show two. Zero has no shaded amount. Keep white-backed fraction tiles
contained and separated from line/ticks; use intrinsic stacked fraction rules.
Do not stretch a bar to the two-whole line width. If simultaneous narrow layout
cannot maintain scale, propose the dossier's explicit-unit sequential presentation
at the gate; do not silently change unit scale by viewport.

Any post-acceptance whole-plus-fraction explanation is brief informational context,
not a learner-earned mixed answer. Omit zero remainder and avoid ambiguous inline
mixed notation. No compulsory decomposition or added reflection. Reduced motion
has the same inspectable static result. Retired controls cannot alter a later item.
