## Goal, reason and scope boundaries

The product has accepted reusable task recipes and direct within-set progression.
Investigate one finite session using existing tasks so responsibility can vary
without making every episode longer. A random queue is not instructional rhythm.
Recommend one small first composition that feels finishable, not an endless course.

Own session purpose, existing-task eligibility/order, transitions, interruption and
stopping semantics. Do not invent new episode tasks, mathematical responsibilities,
representations or whole-plus-fraction construction (Plans54/55), nor redefine
support dimensions, fading or independence categories (Plan56). Use existing support
as fixed explicit configuration. Refer unresolved contracts to orchestration; do
not make other investigators' drafts prerequisites for finishing this dossier.

## Requirement 0 — propose scope and stop

Run preflight. Propose one bounded learner purpose/population assumption, an eligible
task inventory, at most two session outlines and falsifiers. Stop for orchestration
approval before dossier work. Use existing research first. A consequential unresolved
spacing/interleaving/dose question may justify at most three primary studies/guides
with population/task/transfer limits and an approved search bound. Do not turn a
general research effect into an untested FractionFlow schedule or success claim.

## Requirement 1 — two compositions, one recommendation

Compare at most two concrete finite outlines using existing admitted records and
task identities. Prefer a focused concept thread with familiar mechanics, a stable
run and one deliberate responsibility variation or bridge, rather than sampling
every entry-page goal. Explain each transition's mathematical purpose and what
remains familiar. Vary selected dimensions deliberately; do not change denominator
relationship, operation, representation and support all at once.

Specify actual example records and exact truth through existing APIs where needed,
not handwritten solutions. No revalidation of trivial arithmetic by language model.
Keep current raw forms, task bounds and fixed per-episode schedules. Session
ordering does not authorize random omitted beats or a new per-problem code path.
Distinguish prerequisite assumptions, intended learning value and demonstrated
capability; no mastery or independent-transfer claim from completion alone.

Recommend one small pilot composition with a proposed finite dose and explicit
reason, not a universal problem-count/time threshold. Define what evidence could
lead to shortening/changing it. Design a natural optional stop without pressure,
points/streaks, countdowns or manipulative completion mechanics. No new task merely
to fill a rhythm slot. No mandatory mixed retrieval before sufficient focused work.

## Requirement 2 — learner session contract

Describe entry/purpose, existing task launch, acceptance/Next, retry/help, planned
variation, final acknowledgment and return. Show two concrete journeys, including
an interruption/early exit, with concise copy and unmeasured narrow/Chromebook
sketches. Preserve direct progression; do not reintroduce empty Finish, duplicate
instructions or unnecessary interstitial screens. Feedback follows Plan52.

Define retry-same versus next-item identity, exhaustion, ordering reproducibility,
fresh reset and navigation failure. Explain what remains only in the current tab,
what reload loses and what the learner can do after leaving. No silent durable
resume, localStorage, learner profile, analytics or account. First-visit core
practice must remain usable independently of the proposed session feature.

Preserve task-local help, access/motion preferences and focus continuity; session
boundaries must not reclassify assisted work as independent or switch support based
on performance. Different support presentation at a new task must be truthful about
that task's existing capability. No source implementation or automatic policy here.

## Requirement 3 — compact first source recommendation

Recommend data/identity boundaries for one authored session manifest referring to
existing eligible tasks/records, separate from per-episode state and replay. Identify
actual app/progression seams and any missing contract; do not design a universal
curriculum engine. Do not assign schema numbers or alter historical envelopes.
Specify deterministic sequence reconstruction and how each episode retains its
own identity/provenance. Future random selection is a separate admission/design gate.

Give a minimal branch-to-evidence plan: all manifest references/admission, stable
order, no duplicates unless intentional, retry identity, Next/final stop, help/access,
reload/exit and stale controls. Name falsifiers for unwanted auto-advance, lost task
state, incorrect continuation or durable writes. Separate deterministic plumbing,
rendered usability and actual learner engagement/retention evidence.

Recommend owner choices: session purpose/eligible tasks, composition/dose, task
variation, early exit/reload semantics and a first source subset. Record questions
for Plans54–56 without editing their artifacts. No Phase7 exit declaration or Phase8
persistence activation follows from paper approval. Report realistic dependencies
for later implementation, rather than requiring all future math content to finish.
