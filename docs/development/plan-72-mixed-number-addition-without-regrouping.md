## Requirement 2 — One response, with equivalent access

Show the two original mixed numerals with stacked fractions and equal-scale whole and fractional models. Ask for two initially blank counts in one response: **whole units in the sum** and **source-denominator pieces in the sum**. Keep the unit visible and in the field's accessible name. Use one **Check**. The learner determines both totals. The active draft reflects only entered values, distinguishes blank from zero, and preserves input focus and caret. Keep the answer and combined solution model out of the active scene and optional help.

Use neutral guidance for incomplete, invalid or out-of-range responses. Preserve an incorrect draft for revision and give a concise retry. If a bounded draft is numerically equal but organized incorrectly, describe the organization issue accurately. Help may direct attention to the original wholes and pieces without supplying either total. Keep the help event within the attempt through retry, with fresh attempt and replay behavior specified at the mechanism gate. More/Less initially offer the same aids for this task. Keep task-local help available in both.

The linear path presents equivalent operand information, fields, help, retry and accepted result.
