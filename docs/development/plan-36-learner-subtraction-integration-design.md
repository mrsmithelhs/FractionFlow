## Goal and reason

Prepare the first integrated subtraction task, rather than treating subtraction
as addition with a different operator. Plan 15 supplies two accepted operable
prototypes; neither is integrated into learner episodes. Mathematical subtraction
already exists in the exact core. The missing work is instructional and
representational: what the learner does, what removal or comparison means, what
is withheld, how recovery works, and how that task fits reusable authoring.

Recommend an initial **like-denominator takeaway task** asking what remains.
This is a proposal to test against the existing evidence and owner needs, not an
adopted universal preference. Retain comparison for a distinct difference task;
do not add both presentations to every episode. Deliver a concrete design that
can become one bounded implementation packet after acceptance.

## Non-goals and dependencies

No production code, active content admission, prototype alteration, new renderer,
all-family subtraction rollout, negative results, mixed numbers, new arithmetic,
number lines, random selection, adaptation, persistence, learner study or deploy.
No mandatory extra Notice/reflection or multiplication of routine checks.

Plan 15 supplies rendered prototype evidence. Plan 29 supplies purposeful task
variation principles. Plan 35 is an authoring/replay baseline and source-work
serialization dependency, not a mathematical prerequisite for subtraction.
Recheck its delivered contracts before this design starts; do not assume its
proposal is implemented. Later subtraction source work overlaps app/interaction/
render composition and must remain serialized with other source work.

## Requirement 0 — Establish the problem before designing

Run packet preflight and stop if blocked. Produce a source/evidence inventory
and rival-design table. Contrast takeaway for remaining amount, comparison for
distance/difference, and a symbolic-only task where relevant. For each state the
learning purpose, evidence, uncertainty, and observation that would reject the
fit for the first task. Existing mechanical/screen acceptance is not learner
efficacy or a head-to-head preference result. A prototype demonstration alone
cannot falsify a claim about learning.

Propose the smallest task and representation, result-domain boundary, response
responsibility and exact write scope for the dossier. Stop for orchestration
scope confirmation before detailed design. Route a load-bearing new pedagogical
choice through the owner; do not label an implementer recommendation adopted.

## Requirement 1 — One concise, meaningful learner recipe

Specify encounter → learner-controlled subtraction action and response → finish,
or justify a comparably short alternative. Separate the representation action
from the calculation the learner must supply. Do not expose the numeric answer
before the required response; counting a depicted remaining amount can be the
intended mathematical work, but an answer label must not replace that work.
Do not require a gesture merely to acknowledge information already visible.

Specify the stable whole, equal parts, starting amount, removed amount and
remaining amount. Preserve original quantities after removal in an inspectable,
unambiguous way. Explain the same task in the linear view with meaningful learner
participation, rather than a narrative that gives the answer. Remove spent
operation controls and redundant notes, retaining intermediate controls only if
another learner action remains. Keep one local question, concise requested help,
wrong-answer recovery distinct from the prompt, and explicit focus destinations.

Propose actual high/medium behavior only where justified; unchanged support
dimensions need no invented differences. Do not require every past addition
question or final reflection. Specify retry, help open/close, repeated error,
completion, return/re-entry and Next, including preservation of drafts and
truthful assisted provenance. Schedules are authored before responses.

## Requirement 2 — Compact batch and integration contract

Propose a varied like-denominator batch, not a single fixture followed by a
source packet for each numerical change. Use exact algorithms to derive
differences and accepted forms. Vary unit, numerator relationship and remaining
amount; include reducible and irreducible raw differences. Recommend whether an
equal-operands/zero result belongs in this first task or is deferred, with a
specific consequence for rendering and response semantics. Negative differences
remain outside this packet. No subtraction commutativity or operand swapping
that changes the problem's meaning.

Separate mathematical validity, recipe admission, representation capacity and
instructional efficacy. Define compact authored fields, task/definition versions,
effective forms, operation meaning, scene projection, intent/recovery, provenance
and immutable replay contracts. Identify actual addition-specific assumptions by
path and a bounded revision map. Preserve old definition/envelope meanings and
production practice identities. Do not import the standalone prototype wholesale
or introduce per-problem renderer branches. Show which Plan 35 mechanisms can be
reused and which subtraction semantics require explicit new work.

Give a first implementation recommendation with precise files, initial batch,
staged source gates, browser sample, historical/current route treatment and a
later path for nested/unlike subtraction. The later path is a roadmap obligation,
not implicit implementation authority or a demand to design all families now.

## Requirement 3 — Reviewable flow and discriminating validation plan

Provide annotated static states for initial, intermediate/final removal, wrong
response, requested help, correct response and completion in both views. Reuse
stacked notation. Show 360px and Chromebook CSS-layout constraints and enlarged
text; distinguish measured prototype behavior from proposed layout. No prose-only
claim that an unbuilt layout fits. Reuse accepted prototype screenshots honestly.

Plan future checks for exact all-item closure, state/response/provenance/replay,
keyboard and non-drag touch, reduced motion, focus, screen bounds and stable whole.
Name falsification seeds for missing/erased removal hatch, clipped/hidden model,
answer leakage and accidental addition semantics. Require meaningful computed
paint/geometry and hit-testing assertions, not just DOM presence. Retain the
Plan 15 lesson that layout boxes can survive a completely clipped representation.
No physical-device or assistive-technology claims from browser emulation.
