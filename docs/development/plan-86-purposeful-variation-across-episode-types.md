## Requirement 0 — Compare, propose and stop

Pin a committed baseline and inventory existing variation. Distinguish changes of numbers, changes of support or representation, and changes in what the learner actually decides. Start with constructing an equivalent fraction, inspecting and keeping or repairing a supplied form, and identifying which operand needs renaming. Describe when each serves the mathematical goal and where current practice still feels repetitive.

Compare candidates across at least three existing practice families. Recommend up to two new or newly reachable variants across two families for the first pilot. A candidate must keep a clear common mathematical goal, replace or omit existing work, and give the learner a useful decision that remains theirs. Show a matched example before and after, including every required action and Check. Prefer an episode that ends when the learner's work and useful feedback are complete. An app-provided step cannot count as learner work.

Save a concise design and mechanism in `reports/development/plan-86-purposeful-variation-across-episode-types/`. Include matched sketches of active, wrong-answer and accepted views, and what the learner sees or hears before deciding. Specify eligible records, exact validation, stable pattern/record identities, schedule, recovery, help, replay, and deterministic authored exposure for review. Include a concrete candidate the team rejected and why. Stop before source work. The owner approves the task choices and orchestration approves the mechanism and file ownership.

## Implement only the approved pilot

- Reuse the existing recipe and interaction contracts wherever they fit. Keep versioned task patterns separate from mathematical records. Preserve historical identities, accepted paths and truthful provenance for constructed, inspected, repaired and supplied work.
- Use one concise prompt per decision. Show a representation when it supports reasoning and withhold an answer-revealing alignment or endpoint until it has explanatory value. Put optional help beside the decision, with equivalent information and agency in the visual and linear paths. Use house feedback and direct progression. Check rendered narrow and enlarged text, non-drag touch, keyboard, focus and recovery. Show how the variant compares with the current task on the same numbers.
- Keep bank expansion and variation counts separate. A second route through the same problem is instructional variety and does not increase the count of distinct numerical problems. Coordinate with Plan 85 before integrating against shared records, registration or membership. Design may proceed in parallel with Plan 85. Overlapping source integration waits for a released write window and a refreshed baseline.
